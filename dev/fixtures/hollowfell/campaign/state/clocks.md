# Clocks

## Starting clocks

No clocks are running at the campaign's start. The anomalies are escalating, but there is
no specific deadline or countdown in motion.

Clocks will be set in motion as play progresses — when the Unraveler's experiments reach a
critical threshold, when the Covenant makes a move, or when the anomalies threaten a
specific location on a timeline.
