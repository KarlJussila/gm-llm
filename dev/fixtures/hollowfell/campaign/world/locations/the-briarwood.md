# The Briarwood

## Description

The Briarwood is beautiful and wrong. The trees are old — centuries old — and their branches
intertwine into a canopy so thick that the forest floor exists in perpetual twilight. The
bramble is dense and aggressive; paths that were clear yesterday are blocked today. The
light filters through the canopy in strange ways, and travelers report that time moves
differently inside — a journey that should take an hour takes three, or a journey that
should take three takes one.

## Significance

The Briarwood is the site of the first anomaly the PC will encounter. The temporal
disturbances are getting worse, and the Concordance is sending investigators. This is
the campaign's opening investigation — the first thread the PC will pull.

## Notable features

- The Deep Paths — ancient trails through the bramble, some of which lead to places
  that shouldn't exist.
- The Echo Glade — a clearing where sounds from the past can be heard.
- The Thornwall — a barrier of bramble at the forest's edge that seems to grow denser
  when approached with hostile intent.

## Who's here

- Concordance investigators — a small team monitoring the disturbances.
- Local folk who avoid the deep forest.
- The forest itself — the Lattice manipulation is affecting the bramble, making it
  reactive and unpredictable.

## Hooks

- The Concordance needs someone to investigate the temporal disturbances.
- Locals report seeing people in the forest who aren't there.
- A research team went in three days ago and hasn't returned.
- The disturbances are getting worse — something is accelerating them.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden:the-briarwood-1] The Briarwood sits on a Lattice thread — a line of force that the Unraveler
  has been probing. The temporal disturbances are the result of the thread being stressed.
  The Unraveler is using the forest as a testing ground, and the experiments are getting
  more aggressive.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows); [[thaleus-venn]] (suspects).

## Connections

A day's travel east of [[greyhaven]]. The forest borders the [[ashenmere-wastes]] at its
deepest point, though the bramble becomes impenetrable before the Wastes begin.

  reliving the same three hours repeatedly. The Unraveler is observing them.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] only.
