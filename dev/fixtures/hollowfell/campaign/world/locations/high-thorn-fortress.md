# High Thorn Fortress

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Fortress — the Crown's military stronghold on the northern border.
- **Region:** [[crown-of-thorns]]
- **Setting:** Northern highlands, commanding a mountain pass.
- **Controlled by:** [[crown-of-thorns]]
- **Scale:** A large fortress with barracks, armories, and a garrison of several hundred.

## Description

High Thorn Fortress is a stone monolith perched on a rocky outcrop, its walls black with
age and weather. The pass below is the primary route between the Crown and the northern
territories. The fortress is functional and grim — built for war, not for comfort.

## Significance

The fortress guards the Crown's northern border. It is the first line of defense against
incursions from the northern wilds and the last supply point before the frontier.

## Hooks

- The garrison has reported unusual activity in the northern wilds.
- The fortress holds records of the Crown's eastern expansion, including the Marches.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden] The fortress sits on a natural Veil-thin point — a place where the barrier
  between planes is weaker than normal. The Crown does not know this; the fortress was
  built here for strategic reasons, not magical ones. The Unraveler has been probing the
  thin point, and the "unusual activity" the garrison reports is planar bleed from the
  other side.
  Known to: [[unraveler]].
