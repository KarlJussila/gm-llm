# The Convergence Point

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Ruin — the approximate centre of the [[ashenmere-wastes]]; no one has reached
  it in living memory.
- **Region:** [[ashenmere-wastes]]
- **Setting:** The deepest point of the Wastes, accessible only by the most determined
  (or the most foolish) expeditions.
- **Controlled by:** None.
- **Scale:** Unknown — no expedition has returned with accurate descriptions.

## Description

The Convergence Point is the heart of the Ashenmere Wastes — the epicentre of the
Sundering's devastation. No one in living memory has reached it and returned. Expeditions
that approach report increasing distortion: gravity shifts, temporal loops, planar bleed.
The deeper one goes, the worse it gets.

## Significance

The Convergence Point is where the Sundering's damage is deepest. It is also where the
Unraveler's experiments are most advanced — the Lattice wound is deepest here, and the
Unraveler has been exploiting it for centuries.

## Hooks

- Reaching the Convergence Point could yield answers about the Sundering and the Lattice.
- The Unraveler may be based there.
- The Convergence Point is the source of the anomalies spreading across the Wastes.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden] The Convergence Point is not merely the epicentre of the Sundering — it is
  the site of a Lattice node that was nearly destroyed during the catastrophe. The
  Unraveler has been exploiting the damaged node for centuries, using it as an anchor
  for Lattice manipulation. The final experiment — the complete rewrite of the Lattice —
  will be launched from this point. If the node fails before the experiment is ready,
  the collapse will cascade through the entire regional Lattice.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (full); no one else.
