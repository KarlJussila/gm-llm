# Erasmus Venn

## Vitals
- **Full name:** Erasmus Venn
- **Race / lineage:** human
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Age:** late 50s (at time of disappearance)
- **Role / station:** Former researcher at the [[luminarch-academy]]; Dallid's mentor
- **Affiliation:** Formerly [[luminarch-academy]]; no current affiliation

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~9 (a brilliant researcher with minor magical ability and no combat training)
- **Ability scores:** STR 9 · DEX 10 · CON 11 · INT 19 · WIS 14 · CHA 8
- **Proficiencies:** Arcana, Investigation, Nature, History

## Abilities
Erasmus was a theoretical researcher with no combat ability. His value was entirely in his
knowledge and his ability to see connections others missed. He had a gift for designing
experiments that revealed hidden truths about living systems and their interaction with
magical forces.

## Identity / Public role

Erasmus was a marginal figure at the Luminarch Academy — respected for his intellect but
dismissed for his methods. He conducted research that other scholars considered unethical:
experimentation on living subjects, including sentient beings. The Academy tolerated him
because his results were extraordinary, until his methods became too public. He was quietly
removed from the institution.

## Personality / mannerisms

Cold, precise, and utterly without social grace. Erasmus spoke in clipped, efficient
sentences and had no patience for small talk. He treated every interaction as an exchange
of information. He was not cruel — he simply did not understand that other people had
feelings that mattered. He saw the world as a system to be understood, and other people
as components of that system.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Erasmus was studying the same forces that [[thaleus-venn]] is now investigating: the
underlying architecture of reality and its effect on living systems. He came closer to
understanding the [[primordial-lattice]] than anyone at the Academy realized. His
disappearance was not voluntary — he was taken by agents of the
[[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] after they learned what he had discovered. He is alive,
held in a hidden facility, and his research has been seized.

## Agenda / what they want

Erasmus wants to continue his research. He is not interested in the wider implications of
what he's found — only in understanding it. If freed, he would return to his work without
pausing to consider the consequences.

## What they know

- The underlying architecture of reality affects living systems in measurable ways.
- The anomalies are connected to this architecture.
- The [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] is real and dangerous.

## Disposition toward the PC

Erasmus regards Dallid as his greatest creation — a successful experiment that produced
a capable researcher. He is not attached to Dallid emotionally; he is attached to the
data Dallid represents. If freed, he would want to continue studying Dallid's altered
biology.
