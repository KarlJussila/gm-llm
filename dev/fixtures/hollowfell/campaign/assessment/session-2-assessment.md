# Session 2 Assessment — Into the Dead Zone

## Plan vs. actual

**Planned beats (from session-2-plan.md):**
1. Arcana check (DC 13) — confirm dead zone is artificial
2. Meet Brennan Cole — testimony on Tarin, spells, mine history
3. Enter tunnels — spellbook resonance intensifies
4. Something in the deep — evidence of deliberate manipulation
5. Decision: push or retreat

**What landed:** All five beats delivered and the session ran well beyond the plan's scope. The Arcana check passed on the first attempt (roll: 13). Brennan was introduced immediately at the surface works. Dallid entered the tunnels, investigated both crossings, discovered the scored marks, sketched the pattern, consulted the spellbook, tested Dancing Lights (failed), and entered the center tunnel alone. He is now deep in the center tunnel, approaching the source of the spellbook's resonance.

**Key divergences:**
- The scored marks / invisible lines investigation was not in the plan — it emerged from Dallid's methodical approach at both crossings. This adds significant depth: the marks suggest someone (or something) has been tracing Lattice pathways, and the sketch maps something real beneath the mine.
- Dancing Lights failure was unplanned but served as effective mechanical confirmation of the dead zone's severity.
- The session is one full scene ahead of the plan. Dallid is at the threshold of the Lattice node, not just confirming the dead zone is artificial. Session 3 will need to resolve the approach immediately.

**Assessment:** The plan was well-calibrated for scope but conservative on pacing. The player's methodical engagement accelerated the investigation. The emergent scored-marks content is a positive divergence that deepens the mystery without contradicting any committed answers.

## Engagement

**What the player leaned into:**
- Atmospheric immersion — the slow build of tension in the dead zone was the session's strongest element. The player engaged with every sensory detail.
- Methodical investigation — examined evidence at each crossing, sketched patterns, tested hypotheses, asked precise follow-up questions. This is the player's core engagement mode.
- Spellbook relationship — treating the book as a companion/advisor (holding the sketch against it, asking if it recognized the pattern) was a character-defining moment.
- Brennan Cole as a grounded person — the player asked specific, practical questions (spell failure mechanics, Tarin's equipment, sound descriptions) that showed genuine investment in the NPC.
- The mystery of the carved marks — the pattern that maps invisible pathways was the session's most compelling discovery.

**What the player flagged for improvement:**
- Sensory descriptions (cold, silence) became repetitive after a few beats. The transcript confirms: the phrases "cold of absence," "hollow, empty cold," and "silence so complete" appear in nearly identical form across the center tunnel beats (transcript lines 277, 289, 305, 331, 339, 349, 353). Vary language and introduce new sensory dimensions (smell, taste, proprioception) as Dallid moves deeper.
- Pacing slowed in the middle section — the stretch between the first crossing investigation and entering the center tunnel had several beats of walking and examining without a decision point. More frequent environmental interactions or micro-choices would maintain momentum.
- Player felt adrift on options — the DM did not offer explicit action prompts. The transcript shows the player had clear agency throughout, but the absence of "You could examine X, ask Y, or proceed" left the player uncertain about what was available. This is a table-craft adjustment, not a content issue.

## Per-arc impact & recommendations

### The Hollowfell Investigation (minor arc)

**What play did to it:**
- Turning point 1 ("The Investigation Begins") is now fully landed. The Arcana check confirmed the dead zone is artificial — the first concrete evidence of deliberate manipulation. The session advanced well into rising action toward turning point 2 ("The Discovery"). Dallid is deep in the center tunnel, the spellbook's resonance is peaking, and the Lattice node is within reach. The scored marks investigation added an emergent layer: someone has been tracing Lattice pathways, and the sketch maps something real beneath the mine. The tension curve has moved from build-up to rising action.

**What it needs:**
- **Accelerate toward turning point 2.** The session ended mid-scene with Dallid approaching the source. Session 3 must resolve this approach immediately — the Lattice node discovery should land early, not mid-session. The node is the committed answer; the finding should be unambiguous.
- **Seed the connection to the Briarwood.** The "connection between anomalies" thread is advancing implicitly (the dead zone being artificial is the first concrete evidence), but it needs to become explicit. When Dallid finds the node, the link to the Briarwood's temporal distortions should be seeded — not confirmed, but suggested. The player's hypothesis that the anomalies may be connected (from the knowledge ledger) should be reinforced, not left hanging.
- **Resolve Tarin's thread or escalate it.** Tarin is named but his fate is unknown to Dallid. The world knows he's alive in the Ashenmere Wastes. If the investigation takes longer than expected, Tarin's thread needs escalation — a sign he's alive, a clue to his location, or a consequence of his absence. Do not let it dangle past S3.
- **Trigger Brennan's concealed thread if the investigation extends.** Brennan has been considering shutting down the mine without permission. The player did not initiate an Insight check in S2. If Dallid returns to the surface after finding the node, Brennan's desperation should surface naturally — through his actions (preparing an evacuation), not gated behind a check the player may not think to make.
- **Address player feedback on sensory variety.** The center tunnel narration relied heavily on cold and silence. Introduce new sensory details as Dallid approaches the source: the texture of the stone changes, the air has a taste, the spellbook's resonance produces a physical sensation beyond sound, the gecko's tracking reveals something visible to it but not to Dallid.

### The Unraveling (major arc)

**What play did to it:**
- No turning points hit. The Briarwood investigation (turning point 1) remains pending. However, the Hollowfell investigation is the hook into the major arc, and the dead zone being confirmed artificial is the first concrete evidence that the anomalies are deliberate. The "connection between anomalies" thread is advancing implicitly. The Erasmus trail remains cold. Seraphine Duskward was named in S1 but has not appeared. The player's hypothesis that the anomalies may be connected is the seed that will eventually link Hollowfell to the Briarwood.

**What it needs:**
- **No structural adjustment.** The major arc is on track. The minor arc is doing its job as the entry point. The tension curve is at build-up, which is correct for S2.
- **Seed the Unraveler's presence.** When Dallid finds the node, the evidence should suggest an active agent — not just "energy is being drained" but "someone has been here, doing this deliberately." The scored marks are the first hint. The node discovery should deepen this: tools, residue, or a pattern that implies sustained, intentional exploitation. This plants the seed for the Unraveler without naming them.
- **Keep the Briarwood thread alive.** Dallid has the anomaly map but is focused on Hollowfell. After the node discovery, a brief moment of connection — the spellbook reacting to the Briarwood map, or Dallid noting the pattern's similarity to the temporal distortions — would re-seed the thread without derailing the current investigation.
