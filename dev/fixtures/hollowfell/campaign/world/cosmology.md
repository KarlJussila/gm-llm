# Cosmology

## How the world works

Reality is layered. The material world — earth, sky, sea — sits atop a deeper structure that
scholars call the Veil: a permeable boundary between the physical and the planar. Beyond the
Veil lie other planes of existence — the Elemental Depths, the Shroud of Spirits, the Radiant
Court — each a distinct reality with its own rules. Travel between planes is possible but rare,
requiring powerful magic, specific artifacts, or naturally thin Veil-points.

Magic is the manipulation of forces that flow through the Lattice. Sorcerers draw on innate
talent; wizards study and channel through formulae; divine casters channel power through faith
and connection to higher beings. All three are drawing on the same underlying source — they
simply access it differently. The limits of magic are not fully understood; what is known is
that certain areas resist it, certain artifacts amplify it, and certain events disrupt it
catastrophically.

## The deep structure

Beneath the Veil, beneath the planes, beneath everything — there is the Primordial Lattice.

The Lattice is the architecture of existence itself: a web of forces that binds matter to
purpose, souls to bodies, time to sequence. It is not alive in any conventional sense, but
it is not inert — it responds to pressure, adapts to damage, and in rare cases, resonates
with beings who can perceive it. The Lattice is why the world holds together; it is why
magic works; it is why death is not the end.

Only a handful of beings in all of history have perceived the Lattice directly. Most who
glimpse it go mad or simply cannot process what they are seeing. Those who survive the
glimpse carry the knowledge that reality is built on something that can be understood —
and, terrifyingly, something that can be changed.

## The hidden truth

- [hidden] The Primordial Lattice is not merely the architecture of reality — it is
  actively maintained. The Lattice requires periodic stabilization, a function that was
  historically performed by entities or rituals that are now forgotten or suppressed. The
  current age of stability is not natural; it is the result of the last stabilization, which
  occurred during the Sundering and was catastrophic in its execution. The Unraveler has
  discovered that the Lattice can be manipulated for personal ends — rewriting the rules
  of reality in localized areas, bending souls to new purposes, even altering the flow of
  fate. Each anomaly is an experiment in Lattice manipulation. The full scope of what the
  Unraveler is attempting — and what will happen if the Lattice destabilizes completely — is
  not yet known.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (partial); [[unraveler]] (full).

## Implications for play

The Lattice is not something the PC can directly perceive at low levels. It manifests
indirectly: through anomalies, through failed magic, through planar bleed. As the PC
advances, they may develop the ability to sense or interact with Lattice forces — but
this is a gradual progression, not a sudden revelation. The cosmological investigation
that drives the campaign is about piecing together the Lattice's nature from its symptoms,
not about directly studying it.

Divine beings — gods, primordials, ancient cosmic entities — are categorically beyond the
PC's power level. They are forces, not opponents; their influence is felt through their
followers, their artifacts, and their occasional interventions, but they do not appear
as combat encounters. This is a design principle, not a narrative limitation.
