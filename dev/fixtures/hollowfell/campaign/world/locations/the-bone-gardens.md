# The Bone Gardens

## Description

The Bone Gardens are the most disturbing feature of the Ashenmere Wastes. Petrified trees
have twisted into shapes that look like people — figures reaching, screaming, frozen in
agony. The formations are not alive, but they feel wrong. Travelers report hearing whispers
among the petrified figures. The ground is ash-grey and crunches underfoot.

## Significance

The Bone Gardens are a reminder of the Sundering's cost. They are also a source of
unusual magical residue — the petrified figures retain echoes of the life they once held.

## Hooks

- The Bone Gardens contain magical residue that could be studied.
- The whispers are not imagination — something is communicating through the formations.
- An expedition to the Gardens could yield information about the Sundering.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden:the-bone-gardens-1] The petrified figures are not merely echoes — they are souls, trapped in
  the stone by the Sundering's blast. The whispers are their attempts to communicate.
  The Unraveler has been siphoning energy from the trapped souls to fuel experiments
  elsewhere. If the souls were freed, they would carry information about the Sundering
  — and about the Unraveler's role in it.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows and is exploiting); no one else.
