# The Luminarch Academy

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Academy — the continent's foremost institution of magical learning.
- **Region:** [[crown-of-thorns]]
- **Setting:** Located in a fortified city in the western Crown of Thorns, days of travel
  from the capital.
- **Controlled by:** The Academy's own governing body; formally independent of the Crown.
- **Scale:** A large campus: lecture halls, research wings, libraries, dormitories, and a
  central tower. Perhaps five hundred students and staff.

## Description

The Luminarch Academy is an ancient institution — stone buildings draped in ivy, courtyards
where spells are practiced, libraries that smell of old paper and ozone. The central tower
houses the most sensitive research; the lower buildings are lecture halls and dormitories.
The campus is beautiful in a scholarly, timeless way. Magic hangs in the air — not heavy,
but present, like the hum of a machine.

## Significance

The Academy is where Dallid studied and was expelled. It is also the source of much of the
world's magical knowledge — and much of its blind spots. The Academy teaches magic as a
tool, not as a force with underlying principles. Research into those principles is
discouraged.

## Notable features

- The Central Tower — the most restricted area; houses sensitive research and the
  Academy's leadership.
- The Great Library — one of the largest collections of magical texts in the world.
- The Research Wings — where students and faculty conduct experiments.
- The Courtyard of Echoes — an open area where spells are practiced; the stone retains
  magical residue.

## Who's here

- The Academy's governing body — a council of senior mages.
- Students and faculty — hundreds of mages in training.
- No Concordance or Covenant presence — the Academy is fiercely independent.

## Hooks

- Dallid was expelled; his records may still exist. Someone at the Academy knows what
  Erasmus Venn was researching.
- The Academy's Great Library contains texts that might shed light on the anomalies.
- The Academy has a reputation for suppressing dangerous research — what else have they
  buried?

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden] The Academy was founded, in part, to suppress knowledge of the
  [[primordial-lattice]]. The founding charter contains instructions to destroy any
  research that approaches Lattice understanding. The current leadership does not know
  this — the instruction has been passed down as tradition, not as explicit policy.
  Known to: the Academy's oldest archives; [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] (partial).

## Connections

Located in the [[crown-of-thorns]], days of travel from the capital. Connected to
[[greyhaven]] by the long road Dallid traveled when he was expelled.

## Secrets

- [hidden] Erasmus Venn's research notes were seized by the Academy after his disappearance.
  They are stored in the Central Tower. The [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] is aware of
  their existence and has been trying to obtain them.
  Known to: Academy leadership; [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]].
