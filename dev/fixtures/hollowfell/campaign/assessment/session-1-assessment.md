# Session 1 Assessment

## Plan vs. actual

**Plan:** Landing A — Dallid accepts the Hollowfell job, travels to the mine, experiences the dead zone, spellbook reacts. Session ends on the cliffhanger at the tunnel entrance.

**Actual:** Followed Landing A precisely. Every planned beat landed:
- Wren's introduction (the hook) ✓
- Dallid's decision to accept the Hollowfell job ✓
- Travel to Hollowfell Mine ✓
- Arrival and dead zone experience ✓
- Spellbook reaction (cliffhanger) ✓

**Divergences:**
- **Brennan Cole not met** — plan said he might be encountered at arrival; he was held for session 2. No issue.
- **Tarin's disappearance not revealed to Dallid** — plan flagged this as a session 2 beat via Brennan Cole. Correct hold.
- **Briarwood map exchanged in the tavern** — not explicitly in the plan, but a natural extension of the Wren encounter. The player flagged this as slightly too convenient (see engagement).
- **Wren revealed Seraphine Duskward's name unprompted** — plan staged this as only-on-ask. She volunteered it as a parting remark instead. Adds texture; no harm.

**Assessment:** Clean execution of the primary landing. The session hit its structural marks and ended on a strong cliffhanger. The divergences are minor and most improve the fiction.

## Engagement

**What landed:**
1. **Wren's introduction** — the professional directness, the slight crack when she mentioned the Concordance's resource limitations. The player called this "grounded" and said it gave "immediate texture to the world." This is the session's strongest beat.
2. **Layering of mysteries** — Hollowfell as a separate job, the Briarwood as Dallid's own trail, the suggestion they connect. The player said it gave "meaningful choices about where to focus" and "information felt earned rather than dumped."
3. **Spellbook cliffhanger** — sensory details (cold of absence, hollowed air) rather than mechanics. The player called this "perfect" and said it gave Dallid something he'd "have to investigate."

**What felt slightly off:**
- **Wren's map convenience** — the player felt she handed over detailed Briarwood data to a stranger too freely. Suggested she could have been more guarded or offered it as a trade (Briarwood data for Hollowfell findings), reinforcing her professionalism and the relationship's transactional tone.

**Engagement read:** The player is analytical and invested in world-building logic. They lean into sensory narration over mechanics and appreciate NPCs who feel like independent agents with their own agendas. They notice when character behavior breaks established tone (Wren's guardedness vs. the convenient map handoff). Energy was high throughout — no dips. The session held attention from the hook to the cliffhanger.

## Per-arc impact & recommendations

### The Hollowfell Investigation (minor arc)

**What play did to it:**
- Arc moved from dormant to active — Dallid accepted the job and is at the mine entrance.
- The first turning point ("The Investigation Begins") is essentially triggered: the spellbook's reaction to the Lattice node is the first clue the problem is not natural. The Arcana check to confirm this was prepared but not called in session 1 — it is the planned first discovery beat for session 2.
- Dallid has the Briarwood map, which could distract from the Hollowfell investigation. The player accepted the Hollowfell job despite the Briarwood being his primary trail, reasoning the anomalies may be connected. This is good — the connection thread is seeded, not confirmed.

**What it needs — accelerate:**
- Session 2 should deliver the Arcana check (DC 13) as the first concrete discovery: the dead zone is artificial, not natural. The player is at the tunnel entrance and ready to investigate. Delaying this beat would stall momentum.
- Brennan Cole should be met early in session 2. He holds Tarin's disappearance — a beat the player doesn't know is coming but the arc requires. His pragmatic, overwhelmed tone should contrast with Wren's professional distance.
- **Tone correction for Wren's map:** The arc pass should note that Wren's future interactions with Dallid should lean harder into the transactional framing the player suggested. The Briarwood data exchange should be retroactively framed as something Dallid earned through accepting the Hollowfell job, not a freebie. Future Wren scenes should reinforce this — she gives information as payment, not generosity.

### The Unraveling (major arc)

**What play did to it:**
- The Erasmus trail stayed cold, as intended. No one in Greyhaven knows the name. The Briarwood investigation (turning point 1) is correctly held for later levels.
- Dallid revealed Erasmus as his mentor to Wren — a stranger he'd just met. The player framed this as Dallid being analytical and precise, but it's also a notable character beat: Dallid treats people as subjects of study, yet he voluntarily shared personal information. This suggests the character is beginning to open up, or that Wren's competence earned his trust.
- Seraphine Duskward was named. Dallid hasn't met her, but she is now a known entity in the fiction — the First Warden of the Concordance who specifically sent Wren to recruit.

**What it needs — hold and deepen:**
- The Erasmus trail must remain cold through session 2. The Briarwood investigation is the major arc's first turning point (levels 5-6), and it should not be rushed. The Hollowfell investigation should yield evidence that *connects* to the larger pattern, not confirmation of it.
- Dallid's revelation to Wren should be flagged in the next character arc assessment. It's a small beat, but it's the first sign that Dallid's emotional isolation is cracking. The arc pass should ensure this is not forgotten — it matters for the major arc's moral dimension (Dallid learning to value people, not just study them).
- The Hollowfell investigation must connect to the Briarwood through *evidence*, not premature revelation. The Lattice node being drained is the first concrete evidence of deliberate manipulation. The player should discover this at Hollowfell and then realize it might explain the Briarwood's temporal distortions — not the other way around.

### Thread: Concordance Politics

**What play did to it:**
- Seraphine Duskward named as First Warden who sent Wren. She is now a known but unmet figure.
- Wren is reporting to Seraphine about Dallid — this is established in the fiction but not yet a source of tension.
- The Concordance's resource limitations are a background pressure (Wren mentioned the Concordance "doesn't have anyone available").

**What it needs — promote to candidate minor arc:**
- This thread has enough committed answers and turning points to deserve real structure. Seraphine is the PC's primary contact in the major arc; her introduction through Wren's parting remark is good setup. The arc pass should flag this as a candidate for promotion if the player engages with it in session 2 (e.g., asking Wren to relay findings to Seraphine, or Seraphine reaching out directly).
- The tension between the Concordance's institutional caution and Dallid's need for answers should be seeded. Wren's professionalism is the surface; the Concordance's internal politics (the Covenant's influence, the Academy's suppression of Lattice research) are the deeper layer. Hold for now, but the arc pass should be aware this is a live wire.
