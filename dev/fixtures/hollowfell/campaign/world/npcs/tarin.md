# Tarin

## Vitals
- **Full name:** Tarin (surname unknown — a common laborer)
- **Race / lineage:** human
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Age:** early 30s
- **Role / station:** Miner at [[hollowfell-mine]]
- **Affiliation:** Mining consortium backed by the [[crown-of-thorns]]

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** commoner (CR 0) — no combat ability, no magic
- **Ability scores:** STR 13 · DEX 10 · CON 13 · INT 9 · WIS 10 · CHA 9
- **Proficiencies:** Athletics, Perception

## Abilities
None. Tarin is a miner — strong, practical, and uneducated. He knows the deep tunnels
better than anyone except Brennan Cole.

## Identity / Public role

Tarin is a miner who disappeared in the deep tunnels of Hollowfell Mine two weeks ago.
He was one of the experienced workers — he knew the tunnels and was not prone to
carelessness. His disappearance is the incident that forced Brennan Cole to send for the
Concordance.

## Personality / mannerisms

Practical, quiet, dependable. Tarin is the kind of worker who shows up, does the job, and
doesn't complain. He is not educated but he is observant — he notices things other miners
miss. He has a wife and two children in the workers' settlement at Hollowfell.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Tarin stumbled into the Lattice node during a tunnel collapse and was transported to the
Ashenmere Wastes. The transport was an unintended side effect of the Unraveler's siphoning
— the Lattice node discharged when Tarin was too close, and the energy carried him through
to a location a day's travel east. He is alive, confused, and stranded in the Wastes with
no understanding of what happened.

## Agenda / what they want

Get home. Tarin does not understand what happened to him. He knows he was in the mine, then
he was in a rocky wasteland with no food, no water, and no way back. He is trying to
survive and find his way home.

## What they know

- The deep tunnels. He has worked them for months. *(known, firsthand)*
- He was in the mine when the ground opened beneath him. He was pulled through rock. He
  does not understand what happened. *(known, firsthand — but not understood)*
- He is in the Ashenmere Wastes. He does not know where exactly. *(known, firsthand)*
- The Lattice. *(doesn't know)*
- Why he was transported. *(doesn't know)*

## Disposition toward the PC

Grateful and desperate. If Dallid finds him, Tarin will be relieved and cooperative. He
will answer every question because he wants to go home. He does not have useful information
about the Lattice or the anomalies — he is a miner who fell through a hole in reality and
wants his life back.

## Secrets

- [hidden] Tarin was transported by the Lattice node, not by a natural event. He is a
  victim of the Unraveler's siphoning — an unintended casualty.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows); Tarin (does not understand).
