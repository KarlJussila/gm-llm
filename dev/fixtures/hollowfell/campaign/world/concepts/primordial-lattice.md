# The Primordial Lattice

## Nature

The Primordial Lattice is the framework upon which reality is built. It is not a place,
not a being, not a force in the conventional sense — it is the structure that holds
matter, energy, soul, and time together. The Lattice operates on principles that are
not fully understood by anyone; it is ancient beyond reckoning, predating the gods and
the planes.

The Lattice manifests indirectly in the material world. Magic draws on Lattice energy;
the Veil between planes is maintained by Lattice structure; the flow of time is governed
by Lattice rhythm. When the Lattice is stable, reality holds together. When it is
destabilized, reality fractures.

## Perception

Only a handful of beings in history have perceived the Lattice directly. The experience
is described as overwhelming — a sense of the world's underlying architecture, visible
beneath the surface of things. Most who glimpse it go mad or cannot process what they
see. Those who survive carry the knowledge that reality is built on something that can
be understood — and changed.

## Manipulation

The Lattice can be manipulated, but the consequences are unpredictable and often
catastrophic. The Sundering was the result of a failed manipulation — an attempt to
rewrite the Lattice that destroyed entire regions. The Unraveler has discovered more
subtle methods of manipulation, probing the Lattice's resilience without triggering
catastrophic failure — so far.

## Lattice Nodes

Certain locations are Lattice nodes — points where the underlying architecture is
close to the surface. These nodes are powerful and dangerous; they amplify magical
effects, serve as natural planar thin-points, and can be exploited by those who know
how. Several known anomalies cluster around Lattice nodes: [[hollowfell-mine]],
[[the-briarwood]], and others yet undiscovered.

## Implications for play

The Lattice is not something the PC can directly perceive at low levels. It manifests
indirectly: through anomalies, through failed magic, through planar bleed. As the PC
advances, they may develop the ability to sense or interact with Lattice forces — but
this is a gradual progression, not a sudden revelation. The investigation that drives
the campaign is about piecing together the Lattice's nature from its symptoms.
