# Wren

## Vitals
- **Full name:** Wren (surname not given — she uses her single name professionally)
- **Race / lineage:** human
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Age:** late 20s
- **Role / station:** Concordance investigator, field team
- **Affiliation:** [[aetherial-concordance]]

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~4 (a trained investigator with minor magical ability)
- **Ability scores:** STR 10 · DEX 13 · CON 12 · INT 14 · WIS 13 · CHA 12
- **Proficiencies:** Investigation, Insight, Stealth, Perception

## Abilities
Wren carries standard Concordance investigation tools: a badge that grants limited
authority, a set of field supplies, and rudimentary divination magic — she can sense
magical disturbances at close range. She has no combat training beyond basic self-defense.

## Identity / Public role

Wren is a field investigator assigned to the Briarwood monitoring team. She is young,
competent, and ambitious — one of the Concordance's rising operatives. She was sent to
Greyhaven to monitor the Briarwood disturbances and has been there for three weeks. She
reports to Seraphine Duskward, not to the local Concordance outpost.

## Personality / mannerisms

Professional, direct, efficient. She speaks in short sentences and doesn't waste words. She
is not unfriendly but she is not warm — she is here to work. She makes eye contact and
holds it. She has a habit of assessing rooms when she enters them, noting exits and
occupants. She is younger than she looks and more capable than she appears.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Wren was specifically sent to Greyhaven by Seraphine Duskward — not assigned through
normal channels. Seraphine asked her to recruit a capable investigator for the Hollowfell
job because the Concordance council is too slow to act and the anomalies are getting worse.
Wren is loyal to Seraphine and trusts her judgment, but she does not fully understand why
Seraphine is so urgent about this particular job. She follows orders and does not question
them publicly.

## Agenda / what they want

Fill the Hollowfell job. Get a capable investigator to the mine. Report back to Seraphine.
She also wants to prove herself — this is her first solo recruitment assignment and she
wants to succeed.

## What they know

- The mine's deeper tunnels have a magic-dead zone. *(known, secondhand — from the
  consortium's report)*
- A worker disappeared last week. *(known, secondhand)*
- The mining consortium is losing money. *(known, secondhand)*
- The Concordance is stretched thin and can't send anyone from the capital. *(known)*
- Seraphine Duskward specifically asked her to recruit someone. *(known, partially — she
  won't volunteer this unless asked directly)*
- The broader anomaly picture: the Briarwood disturbances are getting worse, Hollowfell
  has problems, scattered reports of planar bleed. *(surface knowledge)*
- Why magic fails in the mine. *(doesn't know)*
- The Lattice. *(doesn't know)*

## Disposition toward the PC

Transactionally hopeful. She has been watching Dallid for a day — he asks the right
questions and he's capable. She needs someone to take the Hollowfell job and she can't go
herself (she's assigned to the Briarwood). If Dallid is interested, she'll make the pitch.
If he pushes for more information than she has, she'll be honest about the limits of her
knowledge.
