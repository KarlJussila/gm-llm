# The Hollowfell Investigation

## Premise

The mining consortium at Hollowfell Mine is losing money and workers are scared — magic
fails in the deeper tunnels, a worker has disappeared, and the mineral seam seems to be
thinning. The Concordance hires Dallid to investigate. What he finds is a Lattice node
being siphoned by an unknown force — the first concrete evidence that the anomalies
spreading across the Marches are deliberate.

## Core conflict & stakes

**Personal:** Dallid is new to Greyhaven and needs the work. The job is straightforward —
find out why magic fails in the mine — but the answers are not. This is his first real
investigation in the Marches, and it will determine whether he stays or moves on.

**Investigation:** Why does magic fail in the mine? What happened to the missing worker?
Is the problem natural or deliberate?

**Connection to the major arc:** The Lattice node is being exploited by the Unraveler.
This is the first time Dallid encounters direct evidence of deliberate manipulation. The
answers he finds here feed directly into the Briarwood investigation and the larger
pattern of anomalies.

## Key NPCs & factions

- **Brennan Cole** — the mine foreman; a practical, overworked man who wants the mine
  working again. He is not a bad person — he's in over his head and knows it.
- **The missing worker** — a miner named Tarin, transported to the Ashenmere Wastes by
  the Lattice node. Alive but confused.
- [[aetherial-concordance]] — the client; provides resources and context. The Concordance
  does not understand what's happening; Dallid's investigation will give them data they
  don't have.

## Turning points

1. **The Investigation Begins** — Dallid enters the mine and experiences the magic-dead
   zone firsthand. His Awakened Spellbook reacts to the Lattice node — the book "sees"
   something Dallid can't perceive directly. The Spellbook's reactions are the first clue
   that the problem is not natural.
   - [revealed:the-hollowfell-investigation-1 S2] The Lattice node is being deliberately drained. The Spellbook can
     sense the drain because it is attuned to magical forces.
   - Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows the node is there); [[dallid]] (confirmed via Arcana
     check S2 — knows energy is being drained, not the agent responsible).
   - **S1:** Partially landed. Dallid arrived at the tunnel entrance, experienced the dead
     zone (cold of absence, hollowed air), and the spellbook reacted involuntarily — a
     persistent resonance with something in the deep tunnels. The Arcana check (DC 13) to
     confirm the dead zone is artificial was prepared but not called.
   - **S2:** Fully landed. Arcana check passed (roll: 13, DC 13) — dead zone confirmed
     artificial; energy is being drained, not suppressed. [[brennan-cole]] introduced at
     surface works; shared full testimony (Tarin's disappearance, spell failure details,
     sounds, mine history, tunnel layout). Dallid investigated both crossings: newer
     scratches at the first crossing follow invisible lines in the stone; scored marks at
     the second crossing map invisible pathways — curves, angles, intersections, a network
     of lines. Sketch confirmed by spellbook as mapping something real. Dancing Lights
     tested and failed — the magic simply isn't there. Dallid entered the center tunnel
     alone. Spellbook resonance intensified from pointed → urgent → peaking. Gecko tracking
     something invisible ahead.

2. **The Discovery** — Dallid finds the Lattice node deep in the mine. He realizes it's
   being drained — not naturally depleted, but siphoned. The missing worker, Tarin, is
   found in the Ashenmere Wastes, confused but alive. He describes being "pulled" through
   the rock.
   - [hidden:the-hollowfell-investigation-2] The Unraveler has been siphoning energy from the node to fuel experiments
     elsewhere. Tarin's transport was an unintended side effect.
   - Known to: [[unraveler]].
   - **S2:** Approaching. Dallid is deep in the center tunnel, past the second crossing.
     The spellbook's resonance is peaking — bone-deep, a frequency just below hearing. His
     gecko is tracking something invisible in the darkness ahead — both heads locked
     forward in "recognition." The cold is unbearable, an absence of something fundamental.
     The center tunnel walls are unworked, scooped out. Session 3 must resolve this

3. **The Connection** — Dallid realizes the mine's problem is connected to the Briarwood's
   temporal disturbances. The anomalies are not random; someone is doing this. This is the
   moment the larger pattern begins to emerge.
   - [hidden:the-hollowfell-investigation-3] The Unraveler's experiments are coordinated. The Briarwood and Hollowfell
     are both test sites.
   - Known to: [[unraveler]]; now [[dallid]] (partially).

## The committed answers

- Why does magic fail in the mine? → A Lattice node beneath the mine is being deliberately
  drained by the Unraveler. The drain creates a dead zone where magical energy cannot
  accumulate.
- What happened to Tarin? → He stumbled into the Lattice node during a collapse and was
  transported to the Ashenmere Wastes. The transport was an unintended side effect of the
  drain.
- Is the problem natural? → No. The anomalies are deliberate. Someone is exploiting the
  Lattice.

## Tension curve

- **Build-up:** Dallid arrives at the mine, experiences the magic-dead zone, begins
  investigating. *(S1–S2 early)*
- **Rising action:** The Arcana check confirms the dead zone is artificial. Brennan's
  testimony. The scored marks at both crossings — a map of invisible Lattice pathways.
  Spellbook confirms the sketch. Dancing Lights fails. Dallid enters the center tunnel
  alone. The resonance peaks. The gecko tracks something ahead. *(S2)*
- **Climax:** Dallid finds the Lattice node and realizes it is being drained — not
  naturally depleted, but siphoned by an active agent. *(approaching — S3)*
- **Falling action:** The immediate problem is contained (the drain is identified), but
  the larger threat is now clear. Dallid has evidence that feeds into the major arc.

## Engagement paths

- **Direct (taken):** Dallid investigated methodically at each crossing — examining
  scratches, scored marks, testing spells, sketching patterns, consulting the spellbook.
  He entered the center tunnel alone, following the resonance to its source. This is the
  route the player is on.
- **Indirect:** Dallid's Spellbook guides him to the truth through its reactions. The
  book "sees" what he cannot, and its behavior leads him to the node. The scored marks
  — someone else's tracing of Lattice pathways — add a second layer: the map is real,
  and someone has been here before.
- **Avoidance:** If Dallid ignores the job, the mine collapses. Workers die. The Lattice
  node is destroyed — making the larger problem worse by removing a data point that could
  have been studied.
- **Alternative solutions:** Dallid could try to stabilize the node (buying time but not
  solving the problem), destroy it (removing the dead zone but losing the evidence), or
  report it to the Concordance (sharing the data but risking a slow institutional response).
