# Brennan Cole

## Vitals
- **Full name:** Brennan Cole
- **Race / lineage:** human
- **Pronouns:** he/him
- **Age:** early 40s
- **Role / station:** Foreman at [[hollowfell-mine]]
- **Affiliation:** Mining consortium backed by the [[crown-of-thorns]]

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~3 (a hardened laborer with no formal training but real-world grit)
- **Ability scores:** STR 14 · DEX 10 · CON 14 · INT 10 · WIS 11 · CHA 9
- **Proficiencies:** Athletics, Perception, Survival

## Abilities
None. Brennan is a practical man with no magic and no combat training beyond swinging a
pickaxe. His value is in his knowledge of the mine and his ability to manage workers under
pressure.

## Identity / Public role

Brennan runs the day-to-day operations at Hollowfell Mine. He is a former miner himself —
promoted to foreman because he was the best worker and the consortium needed someone who
knew the tunnels. He is not a manager by training; he is a miner who got promoted and is
now in over his head. The workers respect him because he's been where they are. The
consortium tolerates him because the mine runs, barely.

## Personality / mannerisms

Blunt, tired, and practical. He speaks in short sentences and doesn't volunteer information.
He answers what's asked and nothing more. He has a miner's directness — no patience for
political language or academic abstraction. He wants solutions, not theories. Under the
pragmatism is genuine concern for his workers — he has lost one and he will not lose
another.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Brennan is in over his head and knows it. He cannot solve the mine's problems — he doesn't
have the knowledge or the resources. He sent for the Concordance weeks ago and is running
out of patience. He is not a bad person; he is a practical man facing a problem beyond his
capabilities, and he will accept help from anyone who offers it, no questions asked. He does
not understand the anomalies or the Lattice; he understands that magic doesn't work in his
mine and workers are disappearing.

## Agenda / what they want

Keep the mine operational. Don't lose any more workers. Get help — anyone who can solve the
problem, because he can't. The consortium is pressuring him for output and he's failing.

## What they know

- The deep tunnels have a magic-dead zone. Spells fail beyond the second crossing. *(known,
  firsthand)*
- A worker named [[tarin]] disappeared last week. No body found. *(known, firsthand)*
- The mineral seam is declining. Output is down. *(known)*
- Workers report strange sounds from deeper in — groaning, like the mountain breathing.
  *(known, firsthand)*
- Why any of this is happening. *(doesn't know)*

## Disposition toward the PC

Relieved and pragmatic. He doesn't care about the Concordance or Dallid's background — he
cares about having someone who can help. If Dallid offers to investigate, Brennan is
grateful and cooperative. He will share everything he knows without being asked, because
holding back information has not helped and he's desperate.

## Secrets

- [hidden] Brennan has been considering shutting the mine down without the consortium's
  permission. He hasn't told anyone. If the investigation fails, he will evacuate the
  workers and accept the consequences.
  Known to: [[brennan-cole]] only.
