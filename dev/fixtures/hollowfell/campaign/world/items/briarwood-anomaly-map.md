# Briarwood Anomaly Map

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Hand-drawn field map — a navigational and intelligence document
- **Rarity:** mundane (no magical properties; its value is informational)
- **Form:** Rough parchment, hand-drawn in ink, folded into a coat pocket. Three locations
  marked with notations in a precise hand.
- **Origin:** Drawn by [[wren]] during her three weeks monitoring the [[the-briarwood]]
  from [[greyhaven]].

## What it is
A rough, hand-drawn map of the Briarwood's three known anomaly locations. The markings are
precise and functional — not artistic. Three sites are labelled and sketched with brief
annotations: the Deep Paths, the Echo Glade, and the Thornwall. The map is small enough to
fold into a coat; the ink is fresh.

## Properties / what it does
No mechanical properties. It is a reference document — a field investigator's working map
of the anomaly sites. It provides direction and context for anyone heading into the
Briarwood to investigate.

## Provenance / history
[[wren]] drew the map during her assignment monitoring the Briarwood temporal distortions.
She gave it to [[dallid]] at the Rusty Anchor in [[greyhaven]] as part of their
arrangement — Briarwood intelligence in exchange for his investigation of the Hollowfell
dead zone.

## Significance
The map is the PC's primary navigational tool for the Briarwood investigation. It marks
the three anomaly sites and implies a spatial relationship between them. The missing
research team was heading for the Echo Glade. The map positions the PC to pursue the
Briarwood thread when ready.

## Deeper layer / true nature
None. The map is what it appears to be — a field investigator's working document. Its
content is accurate to Wren's knowledge, though she has not personally visited all three
sites.
