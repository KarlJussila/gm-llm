## Location
[[thornmere-marches]], foothills east of [[greyhaven]].

## Disposition
Still worked from the surface, under investigation.

## Condition
Magic fails completely past the second crossing — not a fizzle, the magic simply isn't
there. The dead zone is artificial, the energy drawn away deliberately. The cold
is an absence of something fundamental, not just warmth. Scratches at the first crossing
follow invisible lines in the stone. Scored marks at the second crossing map invisible
pathways — a network of intersecting lines. The center tunnel walls are unworked, scooped
out.

## Doing now / immediate intent
The drain goes on. Workers hear a groaning from everywhere at once, worse the deeper they go.
