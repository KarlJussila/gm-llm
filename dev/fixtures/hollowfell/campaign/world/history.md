# World History

## Overview

The world's history is shaped by three great turning points: the Age of Communion, when the
planes were close and magic was abundant; the Sundering, when a catastrophic magical war tore
the Veil and scarred the land; and the Long Recovery, the centuries of rebuilding that
produced the present age. The current era is one of cautious stability — the kingdoms have
recovered, the academies teach, the frontiers are settling — but the scars of the Sundering
are literal and metaphorical, and the knowledge lost in that cataclysm is only now being
rediscovered.

## Timeline

| when | event |
|---|---|
| Age of Communion | The planes were thin and accessible. Magic was widespread. Great civilizations arose that studied the Lattice directly — or believed they did. Their understanding was partial, and their hubris led to catastrophe. |
| The Sundering (~800 years ago) | A coalition of archmages attempted a massive Lattice manipulation to reshape reality. The attempt failed catastrophically: the Veil was torn, entire regions were destroyed or warped, and the planar boundaries destabilized. The Ashenmere Wastes are the largest surviving scar. The archmages were destroyed; their knowledge was lost. |
| The Silence (~700 years ago) | The decades immediately following the Sundering, when magic was unreliable and the Veil was thin. Planar incursions were common. Thousands died. The first Concordance was formed to contain the damage. |
| The Founding (~600 years ago) | The Aetherial Concordance was formally established as a permanent institution to monitor and protect the Veil. The Covenant of the Sealed Veil split from the Concordance during this period, arguing that the Lattice should be forgotten entirely. |
| The Long Recovery (~600–100 years ago) | Centuries of slow rebuilding. Kingdoms reformed, academies were established, the frontier was resettled. Magic became more reliable but less understood — practitioners learned to use it without comprehending its source. The Lattice passed from knowledge into legend. |
| The Present Age (~100 years ago–now) | The world has reached a comfortable equilibrium. The anomalies are new — appearing in the last two decades and accelerating. The Concordance is investigating but has not yet connected the dots. |

## The hidden history

- [hidden] The Sundering was not an accident. The coalition of archmages was manipulated by an
  entity — the same being now known as the Unraveler — who convinced them that the Lattice
  could be rewritten to eliminate death, suffering, and entropy. The Unraveler's true goal was
  to weaken the Lattice's stability so it could be exploited. The Sundering achieved exactly
  that: the Lattice was damaged in ways that made it vulnerable to manipulation. The Unraveler
  has been quietly exploiting that vulnerability ever since, waiting for the Lattice to weaken
  enough for their full plan. The current anomalies are the latest phase.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (partial — he suspects the Sundering was not natural, but lacks
  proof); [[unraveler]] (full).

## Living legacies

The Sundering's scars are everywhere. The Ashenmere Wastes are a blighted region where
reality still doesn't behave correctly. Certain ruins from the Age of Communion contain
artifacts that respond to the Lattice — and some of these artifacts are now malfunctioning
or activating on their own. The Concordance was built to contain Sundering-damage; it now
faces a threat it was not designed for. The Covenant of the Sealed Veil was born from the
Sundering's lesson — that some knowledge is too dangerous — and its members will go to
extreme lengths to prevent a second cataclysm. The common folk remember the Sundering as
a myth, a warning, a reason to fear magic.
