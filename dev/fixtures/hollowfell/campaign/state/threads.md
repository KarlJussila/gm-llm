# Active Threads

## Starting threads

These are the leads Dallid begins with, drawn from his backstory and the opening situation.

### The Erasmus Venn trail
- **What:** Dallid came to the Marches to find his missing mentor, [[erasmus-venn]].
  The last lead pointed to [[the-briarwood]].
- **Status:** Cold. No one in Greyhaven has heard of Erasmus. The Concordance erased his
  records. No new information in S2.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-unraveling]] (major arc)

## Threads advanced in S1–S2

### Hollowfell dead zone
- **What:** Magic fails in the deeper tunnels of [[hollowfell-mine]]. Spells fail, even
  cantrips. A worker vanished last week. The mining consortium is hemorrhaging money.
- **Status:** Active. The dead zone is **confirmed artificial** (S2, [[dallid]] Arcana check
  DC13) — the energy is being drained, not suppressed. Someone is doing this deliberately.
  [[dallid]] is deep in the center tunnel, past the second crossing, following the
  spellbook's resonance to its source.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc), [[the-unraveling]] (major arc)

### Tarin's disappearance
- **What:** A worker at [[hollowfell-mine]] vanished in the deep tunnels nine days ago.
  [[brennan-cole]] shared the details in S2.
- **Status:** Partially resolved (S2). Tarin is named. Experienced miner, knew the tunnels
  better than anyone except Brennan. His lantern was found at the second crossing, still
  lit. He went into the center tunnel. His fate beyond that is unknown to [[dallid]]. (The
  world knows he is alive in the [[ashenmere-wastes]], transported by the Lattice node.)
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)

### Briarwood anomalies
- **What:** The [[the-briarwood]] has temporal distortions — time moves differently inside
  the forest. Three anomaly locations marked on a map [[wren]] gave to [[dallid]]: the
  Deep Paths (ancient trails leading places that shouldn't exist), the Echo Glade (sounds
  from the past), the Thornwall (a barrier that grows denser with hostile intent). A
  research team (2 humans, 1 half-elf cartographer, 1 marsh-hamlet guide) went to the
  Echo Glade 3 days ago and hasn't returned.
- **Status:** Dormant (no advancement in S2). [[dallid]] has the [[briarwood-anomaly-map]]
  but is focused on [[hollowfell-mine]]. Queued for after the Hollowfell investigation.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-unraveling]] (major arc — turning point 1 is the Briarwood
  investigation)

### Connection between anomalies
- **What:** The anomalies across the [[thornmere-marches]] may be connected — not random,
  but coordinated. [[hollowfell-mine]] and [[the-briarwood]] are both affected.
- **Status:** Advancing (implicit). The dead zone at [[hollowfell-mine]] being confirmed
  artificial is the first concrete evidence of deliberate [[primordial-lattice]]
  manipulation — the same force affecting the Briarwood may be behind this. Not yet
  explicit to [[dallid]].
- **Hooks into:** [[the-unraveling]] (major arc)

## Threads seeded in S1, advanced in S2

### Scored marks / invisible lines
- **What:** Scratches at the first crossing follow invisible lines in the stone. Scored
  marks at the second crossing map invisible pathways — curves, angles, intersections, a
  network of lines. Someone (or something) has been tracing Lattice pathways that are
  invisible to normal sight.
- **Status:** Active (S2). [[dallid]] investigated both crossings and sketched the pattern.
  The sketch was confirmed by the spellbook as mapping something real beneath the mine.
  Who made the marks is unknown.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)

### The gecko's recognition
- **What:** [[dallid]]'s two-headed gecko companion is tracking something invisible in the
  darkness of the center tunnel. Its behavior shifted from curiosity to recognition — both
  heads locked forward, rigid, watching something [[dallid]] cannot see.
- **Status:** Active (S2). The gecko went rigid at session's end. It is sensing something
  ahead — likely related to the Lattice node or the source of the spellbook's resonance.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)

### Spellbook resonance
- **What:** [[dallid]]'s Awakened Spellbook has been reacting to something in the deep
  tunnels — a persistent resonance with the Lattice node beneath the mine.
- **Status:** Peaking (S2). Resonance built from pointed (S1) → urgent → bone-deep, a
  frequency just below hearing (S2). Confirmed the sketch's pattern relates to what it
  senses. The source is close.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)

### Brennan's secret
- **What:** [[brennan-cole]] has been considering shutting down the mine without the
  consortium's permission. He hasn't told anyone. If the investigation fails, he will
  evacuate the workers and accept the consequences.
- **Status:** Seeded (not triggered in S2). The player did not initiate an Insight check on
  Brennan. The thread should surface through his actions (preparing an evacuation) if
  [[dallid]] returns to the surface, not gated behind a check.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)
