# The Unraveler

## Identity / Public face

The Unraveler has no public face. They are not known to exist. The anomalies are attributed
to natural causes, to the Sundering's lingering effects, or to unrelated magical
disturbances. The Unraveler's agents operate through intermediaries — hiring mercenaries,
corrupting researchers, manipulating factions — and never reveal their employer.

## Goals & methods

The Unraveler's goal is to manipulate the Primordial Lattice to reshape reality according
to their will. The full scope of their ambition is not yet known — it may be unlimited.
Their methods include:
- Probing the Lattice through localized experiments (the anomalies).
- Exploiting Lattice nodes to siphon energy and weaken stability.
- Manipulating factions and individuals to create conditions favorable to their work.
- Suppressing anyone who gets close to understanding what is happening.

## Structure & key members

The Unraveler operates through layers of intermediaries. The inner circle — those who know
the Unraveler's identity and true purpose — is very small. Below them are operatives who
carry out specific tasks without understanding the larger picture. The structure is designed
to be resilient: if one layer is exposed, the layers above remain hidden.

## Reach & resources

The Unraveler has been operating for centuries and has accumulated significant resources:
artifacts, knowledge, and a network of pawns in every major faction. Their greatest
resource is time — they have been playing a very long game.

## Deeper layer / true agenda

- [hidden:unraveler-1] The Unraveler is not merely exploiting the Lattice — they are attempting to
  overwrite it. The Lattice is the architecture of reality; the Unraveler intends to
  rewrite that architecture to make reality conform to their design. The anomalies are
  experiments in Lattice manipulation, each one probing the Lattice's resilience and
  mapping its weaknesses. The Unraveler's ultimate goal is a complete rewrite — a new
  reality, designed from the ground up.
  Known to: the Unraveler (full); their inner circle (partial).

## Relationships

- [[aetherial-concordance]] — The Unraveler has been corrupting the Concordance's monitoring
  network, creating blind spots around their operations. They consider the Concordance a
  nuisance but not a serious threat.
- [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] — The Unraveler is aware of the Covenant and considers
  them a potential threat — not because of their current capabilities, but because of
  their knowledge.
- [[thaleus-venn]] — The Unraveler is aware that Venn is getting close to the truth and
  has been working to discredit or eliminate him.

## Disposition toward the PC

The Unraveler does not know the PC exists at the campaign's start. As the PC becomes
involved in the anomalies, the Unraveler will become aware of them and assess the threat.
If the PC proves capable, the Unraveler will attempt to manipulate, misdirect, or
eliminate them.

## Secrets

- [hidden:unraveler-2] The Unraveler was the entity that manipulated the archmages into triggering the
  Sundering. The Sundering was not an accident — it was the Unraveler's first successful
  experiment in large-scale Lattice manipulation. The resulting damage created the conditions
  the Unraveler has been exploiting ever since.
  Known to: the Unraveler only.
- [hidden:unraveler-3] The Unraveler's full plan requires one more large-scale experiment before the
  rewrite can begin. The current anomalies are the preparation. The final experiment will
  be catastrophic — potentially destructive on a continental scale.
  Known to: the Unraveler; their inner circle (partial).
