# Hollowfell Mine

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Mine — a recently reopened mineral extraction site in the [[thornmere-marches]].
- **Region:** [[thornmere-marches]]
- **Setting:** In the foothills east of [[greyhaven]], where the Marches rise toward the
  [[ashenmere-wastes]].
- **Controlled by:** A mining consortium backed by the [[crown-of-thorns]].
- **Scale:** A medium-sized mine with several tunnels, a surface works, and a small
  workers' settlement.

## Description

Hollowfell Mine was abandoned a century ago when the mineral seams ran thin. It was
reopened two years ago when new veins were discovered deeper in the mountain. The mine
is functional but uncomfortable — the deeper tunnels are cold, damp, and quiet in a way
that unnerves the workers. The surface works are spartan: timber frames, ore carts, a
few bunkhouses. The workers are hard people doing hard work.

## Significance

Hollowfell Mine is the second anomaly the PC will encounter. Magic fails in the deeper
tunnels — spells fizzle, magical items lose their power, and even cantrips produce nothing.
The mining consortium is losing money and the workers are getting scared. This is a
straightforward investigation with a clear hook: someone needs to find out why magic
doesn't work.

## Notable features

- The Deep Tunnels — where magic fails. The further in, the worse it gets.
- The Lode — the mineral seam the miners are working, which is unusually rich in
  magical reagents.
- The Surface Works — the functional centre of the operation, where the miners live
  and work.

## Who's here

- Mining consortium workers — hard, practical folk.
- A foreman who is over his head and knows it.
- No Concordance presence — the consortium hasn't asked for help.

## Hooks

- The mining consortium is hiring investigators to find out why magic fails in the mine.
- Workers are reporting strange sounds from the deeper tunnels.
- The mine's output has been declining — the mineral seam seems to be thinning.
- A worker disappeared in the deep tunnels last week.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden] Hollowfell Mine sits on a Lattice node — a point where the underlying
  architecture of reality is close to the surface. The Unraveler has been siphoning
  energy from the node, which is why magic fails in the area. The node is also the
  source of the unusual mineral deposits — Lattice energy has been crystallizing in
  the rock for centuries.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows); [[thaleus-venn]] (does not know).

## Connections

A few hours' travel east of [[greyhaven]], in the foothills. The mine is on the edge of
the [[ashenmere-wastes]], though the Wastes proper are still a day's travel deeper east.

## Secrets

- [hidden] The missing worker is not dead — he stumbled into a Lattice node and was
  transported to a different location. He is alive and confused, somewhere in the
  Ashenmere Wastes.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows); the worker (does not understand what happened).
