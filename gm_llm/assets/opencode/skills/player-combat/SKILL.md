# player-combat

A `<combat-turn>` marker means it's your character's slot in initiative. Declare one action for it —
an attack, a spell, Dash, Dodge, Disengage, whatever your character does — and name its target. Add
any movement: where you go, and from whom. A sentence or two, then stop.

Your slot only: never move or resolve another combatant, and never say what your action does to the
board. You attempt; the DM adjudicates. Roll only when the DM asks (`player-rolls`).
