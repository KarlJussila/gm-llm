# World Overview

## Premise

A grounded world of frontier towns, ancient academies, and recovering kingdoms, where magic is
real but not understood — practiced by few, feared by many, and studied by fewer still. The
cosmos operates on hidden principles that even scholars have only glimpsed. The campaign opens
on the edges of civilization, where an unknown adventurer takes local work for coin, far from
the centres of power that will eventually demand their attention.

## The central conflict

Reality is fracturing. Across the world, places are phasing between states, magical artifacts
are failing or behaving in impossible ways, and the boundaries between planes are thinning.
The Aetherial Concordance — the ancient order sworn to maintain cosmic balance — is struggling
to contain the spreading corruption. Political factions jostle for advantage in the chaos, and
old grudges resurface as the world's foundations groan. Beneath all of it, someone is
deliberately exploiting the hidden architecture of reality, and the full scope of their plan
won't become clear until catastrophe is nearly upon the world.

## The surface situation

The world appears stable — kingdoms trade, academies teach, adventurers take jobs. But
cracks are showing. Strange phenomena cluster in certain regions: a forest where time moves
at the wrong speed, a ruin that reappears in different locations, a mine where magic simply
stops working. The Concordance sends investigators; scholars debate causes; the common folk
blame gods or bad omens. The symptoms seem disconnected, each a local problem with a local
explanation. Nobody has yet drawn the lines between them.

## The hidden layer

- [hidden] The Primordial Lattice — the hidden architecture underlying all of reality — is
  being deliberately exploited. A being known only as the Unraveler has discovered how to
  manipulate the Lattice's threads, bending fate, souls, and planar boundaries to their will.
  The anomalies are not random; they are the Unraveler's experiments, each one probing the
  Lattice's resilience and mapping its weaknesses. The Unraveler's identity and ultimate
  goal remain concealed; their agents operate through intermediaries, and their true scope
  is not yet legible.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (partially — he has glimpsed the pattern but not the agent);
  the Unraveler and their inner circle (fully).

## Themes & tone

The campaign is about the cost of knowledge, the weight of responsibility, and the
fragility of foundations — both personal and cosmic. It begins with wonder: discovering a
richly built world, taking on challenges that feel achievable, earning a reputation. It
progressively grows heavier as the scope expands and the consequences of what's being
uncovered become real. Heroic wonder meets genuine danger; the PC should feel capable but
never invincible, and the gods should feel categorically beyond reach — a different order
of magnitude, not just stronger opponents. Clever investigation is always rewarded; brute
force is sometimes necessary but never sufficient.

## Factions at a glance

- [[aetherial-concordance]] — ancient order sworn to protect reality's foundations; struggling
  to contain the spreading anomalies.
- [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] — secretive faction that believes certain knowledge should
  never be uncovered; will destroy what they cannot control.
- [[unraveler]] — hidden antagonist exploiting the Primordial Lattice; identity and scope unknown
  to the world at large.
