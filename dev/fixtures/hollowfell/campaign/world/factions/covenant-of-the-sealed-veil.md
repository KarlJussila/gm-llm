# The Covenant of the Sealed Veil

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Secret society — part religious order, part militant academy. Cells operate
  independently; leadership is hidden even from most members.
- **Scale:** Small but widespread — perhaps three hundred active members, organized in cells
  of five to ten. Present in every major region but never openly.
- **Seat:** Unknown — the Covenant has no fixed headquarters. Leadership operates from
  hidden sanctums.
- **Leadership:** A council of three Veilkeepers, whose identities are secret. The most
  visible is [[vashra-keel]], who handles field operations.
- **Founded:** ~600 years ago, when the first Veilkeeper broke from the Concordance.

## Identity / Public face

The Covenant does not have a public face. Most people have never heard of them. Those
who know of them consider them a myth, a conspiracy theory, or a vanished cult. They
recruit selectively, operate covertly, and disappear when their work is done.

## Goals & methods

The Covenant's stated goal — among its own members — is to prevent another Sundering by
any means necessary. Their methods include destroying dangerous artifacts, suppressing
dangerous knowledge, eliminating individuals who threaten the Veil, and — when necessary —
sabotaging organizations they consider reckless. They are ruthless, pragmatic, and
genuinely convinced that they are saving the world.

## Structure & key members

Cells are semi-autonomous; each cell operates independently, with minimal knowledge of
other cells. Communication between cells is rare and carefully managed. Key members:
- [[vashra-keel]] — Field commander; the most visible leader.
- The three Veilkeepers — the hidden council; identities unknown.

## Reach & resources

The Covenant's strength is secrecy and conviction. They have infiltrated the Concordance,
the Crown's court, and various academic institutions. They can mobilize small teams of
highly trained operatives for targeted operations. Their weakness is scale — they are too
small to control events, only to influence them.

## Deeper layer / true agenda

The Covenant believes that the Primordial Lattice is real and that it is inherently
dangerous — that any attempt to study or manipulate it will lead to catastrophe. Their
goal is not merely to prevent a second Sundering but to ensure that knowledge of the
Lattice is destroyed entirely. They are willing to kill to achieve this, and they consider
the Concordance's research to be as dangerous as the Unraveler's manipulation.

## Relationships

- [[aetherial-concordance]] — Estranged allies; the Covenant considers the Concordance
  dangerously reckless.
- [[crown-of-thorns]] — The Covenant has agents in the court but no formal relationship.
- [[unraveler]] — The Covenant is aware that something is manipulating reality but does not
  yet know the Unraveler's identity or scope.

## Disposition toward the PC

The Covenant will observe the PC before making contact. If they determine the PC is a
threat — particularly if the PC shows interest in Lattice-related phenomena — they will
take action to stop them. If the PC proves aligned with their goals, they may offer
information and assistance, but always at a price.

## Secrets

- [hidden] The Covenant's founders were the last people to fully understand the Lattice.
  They chose to suppress the knowledge rather than protect it, believing that the Lattice
  is too dangerous to be studied by anyone.
  Known to: the Veilkeepers (full); [[vashra-keel]] (partial).
- [hidden] The Covenant has a contingency plan: if the Lattice destabilizes beyond repair,
  they intend to trigger a controlled collapse that would seal the Lattice permanently —
  destroying all magic in the process. They consider this an acceptable cost.
  Known to: the Veilkeepers only.
