# Campaign

## Premise

A long-form investigation-driven campaign spanning levels 5–20, set in a grounded world
of frontier towns, ancient academies, and recovering kingdoms. The PC begins as a nobody —
an unknown adventurer doing local work for coin, with no powerful allies and no sense of
the bigger picture. The campaign builds gradually from local anomalies to world-shaking
stakes, driven by investigation, intrigue, and discovery.

## Setting

An original world built for slow discovery — grounded, detailed, and richly layered. Magic
is real but not fully understood. The cosmos operates on hidden principles that even scholars
have only glimpsed. Gods, primordials, and ancient cosmic entities are categorically beyond
the PC's power — a different order of magnitude, not just stronger opponents.

The campaign begins in the [[thornmere-marches]], a frontier province of marshes, bramble
forests, and scattered settlements. The nearest significant town is [[greyhaven]], where
the [[aetherial-concordance]] maintains a small outpost. Strange phenomena are spreading
across the region — temporal disturbances in the [[the-briarwood]], magic failing in
[[hollowfell-mine]], and scattered reports of planar bleed. The symptoms seem disconnected.
Nobody has yet drawn the lines between them.

## Tone

Heroic wonder with real consequences, starting lighter and growing heavier as the story
escalates. The early game is grounded and exploratory — taking jobs, earning reputation,
discovering a richly built world. The mid game grows more complex — political intrigue,
factional pressure, investigation into the anomalies' cause. The late game is
consequential and weighty — the scope expands to world-shaking stakes, and the cost of
knowledge becomes real.

Investigation is the primary driver — arcane and cosmological investigation especially,
but also political intrigue, heists, research, social encounters, and exploration. Combat
is present but should be meaningful and thematic; cleverly avoiding minor encounters is
rewarded, though climactic fights remain unavoidable.

## Themes

- **Knowledge as power and its cost.** Understanding the world's foundations comes with
  responsibility and danger.
- **The fragility of foundations.** Personal, political, and cosmic stability are all more
  precarious than they appear.
- **Investigation and discovery.** The drive to understand, to connect the dots, to find
  the truth beneath the surface.
- **Escalating stakes.** From local problems to world-shaking consequences, the weight
  of what's at stake grows steadily.

## Structure

The campaign is structured around a gradual escalation:

- **Levels 5–8:** Local anomalies. The PC takes investigation work from the Concordance,
  encountering the symptoms of Lattice manipulation without understanding their cause.
- **Levels 9–13:** Regional scope. The anomalies connect. Political pressure mounts. The
  PC becomes entangled with multiple factions, each with their own agenda. Power shift:
  the PC feels substantial starting around level 12+.
- **Levels 14–20:** World-shaking stakes. The full pattern becomes legible. The Unraveler's
  plan is revealed. The PC must act against a threat that has been building for centuries.

## Content boundaries

Anything goes as long as it serves a purpose.

## Key entities

- [[aetherial-concordance]] — ancient order investigating the anomalies.
- [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] — secret society suppressing dangerous knowledge.
- [[unraveler]] — hidden antagonist exploiting the [[primordial-lattice]].
- [[seraphine-duskward]] — Concordance First Warden; the PC's primary contact.
- [[thaleus-venn]] — the only person studying the Lattice.
- [[vashra-keel]] — Covenant field commander; ruthless and pragmatic.
