# The Sundering

## What it was

The Sundering was a magical catastrophe that occurred approximately 800 years ago. A
coalition of archmages from the Age of Communion attempted a massive Lattice manipulation
— an effort to rewrite the rules of reality itself. The attempt failed catastrophically:
the Veil between planes was torn, entire regions were destroyed or warped, and the
planar boundaries destabilized. The Ashenmere Wastes are the largest surviving scar.

The Sundering killed thousands, destroyed civilizations, and set the world back centuries.
It also suppressed knowledge of the Lattice — the archmages who understood it were
destroyed, and their work was lost.

## The official account

The official account — taught in academies and accepted by most scholars — is that the
archmages were hubristic, that their experiment was reckless, and that the Sundering was
a natural consequence of overreaching. This account is not wrong, but it is incomplete.

## The hidden truth

- [hidden:the-sundering-1] The Sundering was not an accident. The archmages were manipulated by the
  [[unraveler]], who convinced them that the Lattice could be rewritten to eliminate
  death, suffering, and entropy. The Unraveler's true goal was to weaken the Lattice's
  stability so it could be exploited. The Sundering achieved exactly that: the Lattice
  was damaged in ways that made it vulnerable to manipulation.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (full); [[thaleus-venn]] (suspects).

## Living legacies

The Sundering's scars are everywhere. The Ashenmere Wastes are a blighted region where
reality still doesn't behave correctly. Certain ruins from the Age of Communion contain
artifacts that respond to the Lattice. The [[aetherial-concordance]] was built to contain
Sundering-damage. The [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] was born from the Sundering's
lesson — that some knowledge is too dangerous.
