# Session 2 — Into the Dead Zone

## Narrative

1. **Surface works encounter.** Dallid turns from the tunnel mouth and walks toward a figure moving between the bunkhouses. He calls out about the tunnel's unusual resonance. The figure is **Brennan Cole** — foreman at Hollowfell Mine, broad-shouldered, worn leather coat, visibly exhausted. Brennan assumes Dallid is the Concordance investigator he sent for weeks ago. Dallid corrects him: he is a scholar studying anomalous phenomena, not from the Concordance.

2. **Brennan's testimony.** Brennan shares what he knows: spells fail past the second crossing — all of them, cantrips, wards, everything. A worker named **Tarin** disappeared nine days ago. He was experienced, knew the tunnels better than anyone except Brennan. His lantern was found at the second crossing, still lit. Others have gone deep and returned shaken but alive. The ones who refuse heard groaning sounds — "like the mountain breathing" — and it gets worse the deeper you go. The cold is not like winter; it is like "something's been taken away."

3. **Tunnel layout.** Brennan describes the layout: main tunnel goes ~200 feet to the **first crossing** (side tunnels branch off, dead ends used for storage). Another 100 feet to the **second crossing** — that is where the cold starts and spells stop working. Beyond the second crossing, three branches: left goes to the main lode (where ore is being pulled), right narrows and is unstable, **center goes straight back** — that is where Tarin went.

4. **Brennan offers to guide.** Brennan offers to take Dallid as far as the second crossing but will not go deeper. He has workers to keep alive. Dallid accepts.

5. **Threshold examination.** At the tunnel entrance, Dallid asks Brennan to hold the lantern steady while he examines the transition point. He reaches for his knowledge of magical suppression, planar interference, energy displacement. **Arcana check: 3 + 10 = 13** (passed DC 13).

6. **The dead zone is artificial.** Dallid determines: the magical field is not gone — it is being *drained*. Not natural suppression, not a dead magic zone, not a planar ward. The energy is here but pulled away, drawn down into the earth like water into a sinkhole. He concludes aloud: "It's not a dead magic zone. The energy is being drained. Deliberately. Someone is doing this." Brennan receives this grimly.

7. **Journey to the second crossing.** Brennan leads Dallid into the tunnel. The cold deepens with every step. The lantern light barely reaches ten feet. They pass the first crossing (~200 feet in) — Dallid pauses to examine the junction.

8. **First crossing investigation.** Dallid kneels at the junction, tracing the base of the wall. He finds tool marks (old), boot prints (some fresh), and **scratches in the stone** — deep, deliberate, not mining marks. **Investigation check: 6 + 7 = 13** (passed). The newer scratches follow *invisible lines in the stone* — as if someone was tracing something they could see but Dallid cannot. Brennan confirms neither he nor his workers made them; no one has been past the first crossing since the cold started. Dallid tells Brennan the evidence suggests recent activity by someone other than the workers.

9. **Second crossing.** Brennan leads Dallid the remaining 100 feet. The cold drops suddenly, "as if you've passed through a membrane into a different place." They reach the three-way split: left (main lode, wider, newer timber), right (narrow, partially collapsed), **center** (straight back into darkness). Brennan stops here: "This is as far as I go." He will be at the surface when Dallid returns. He departs.

10. **Spell fails.** Dallid attempts to cast **Dancing Lights** at the second crossing. The spell never begins — "not a fizzle, not a spark that dies. The magic simply isn't there." He reaches for it and finds a void. The spellbook pulses harder, faster, as if trying to compensate.

11. **Second crossing marks.** Dallid kneels at the threshold and examines the stone where the cold intensifies. He finds **scored marks** — faint lines carved with something sharp but not a tool. They mirror the invisible lines from the first crossing but are clearer, more deliberate: curves, angles, intersections. **Investigation check: 5 + 7 = 12** (passed). The pattern looks like a map. The stone is hollow — energetically scooped out, the magical field drained. His gecko leans forward, tracking something he cannot see.

12. **The sketch.** Dallid pulls out his notebook and sketches the scored pattern: a network of lines, intersecting and diverging, following invisible pathways in the rock. "This looks deliberate. A map, or a diagram of some kind."

13. **Consulting the spellbook.** Dallid holds the notebook against the spellbook and asks if it recognizes the pattern. The spellbook warms — not the cold pulse of resonance, but something different. He feels a certainty: the pattern in the stone is related to what the book senses deeper in the mine. The lines follow the same invisible pathways the book has been pointing toward. The book pulses once, hard, confirming. The sketch maps something real.

14. **Entering the center tunnel.** Dallid tells Brennan the pattern is real and maps something beneath the mine. He steps into the center tunnel alone. Brennan remains at the second crossing.

15. **The deep dead zone.** The cold is different here — "absence of something fundamental." The air is thin, hollow. Footsteps don't echo; sound dies the moment it leaves his boots. The walls are rough, unworked, unmarked — "as if the tunnel was not carved but scooped out." The spellbook pulses harder, faster, the resonance urgent and spreading.

16. **Gecko tracking.** The gecko leans forward, both heads turning slowly, tracking something Dallid cannot see. Its claws tighten on his shoulder. Its attention is fixed on a point ahead — not sound, not movement, but *something*. Then it goes completely still — both heads, locked forward. Not watching. Recognizing.

17. **Perception attempt.** Dallid stops, holds his breath, and listens. **Perception check: 3 + 1 = 4** (failed). The silence is absolute — "not the silence of empty tunnels, but a silence that feels like something is pressing down on it, smothering sound before it can form." He catches nothing. The silence is too complete.

18. **Pressing deeper.** Dallid continues forward. The cold deepens with every step — "that hollow, empty cold that feels like the world is being erased." The spellbook's resonance peaks — "a frequency that vibrates in your bones, just below hearing." The gecko goes rigid, both heads locked forward, watching something Dallid cannot see.

19. **Session ends.** "Something shifts ahead. Not a sound. Not a movement. An absence — a silence so complete it feels like the world is holding its breath." The source is close. The spellbook is certain. Something waits in the darkness ahead. Dallid is deep in the center tunnel, past the second crossing.

20. **Player feedback.** (Collected post-session — see Player Feedback section below.)
