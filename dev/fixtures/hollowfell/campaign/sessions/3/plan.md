# Session 3 Plan — The Source

## Opening State

**Where:** [[hollowfell-mine]], deep in the center tunnel, past the second crossing. Walls
are rough, unworked — "as if the tunnel was not carved but scooped out." The cold is an
absence of something fundamental. Sound dies the moment it leaves your boots.

**When:** Night, continuing from S2. [[dallid]] has been underground for hours.

**Who:** [[dallid]], alone in the center tunnel. His gecko is rigid on his shoulder, both
heads locked forward. [[brennan-cole]] is at the surface works, waiting.

**Immediate situation:** The spellbook's resonance is peaking — bone-deep, a frequency just
below hearing. The gecko is tracking something invisible ahead. Something shifts in the
darkness — not a sound, not a movement. An absence. The source is close. S3 opens on this
exact moment; the cliffhanger resolves immediately.

## Likely Beats

1. **The approach and discovery.** Dallid moves forward. The gecko leads. The source
   reveals itself — not a dramatic reveal, but a wrongness in the stone that resolves into
   something real. This is the Lattice node. *(lands within the first10–15 minutes; do not
   delay.)*

2. **Evidence of exploitation.** The node shows signs of deliberate, sustained siphoning —
   not a natural drain but an engineered one. Someone has been here, doing this, for
   months. This deepens the mystery from "why does magic fail" to "who is doing this and
   how."

3. **Tarin's trace.** Something at the node connects to [[tarin]]'s disappearance — a
   scuff mark, a dropped tool, disturbed dust, a spot where the stone is worn from
   passage. The trace suggests Tarin was *here* and is no longer here. It does not answer
   where he went — only that he reached this place and was removed.

4. **Briarwood connection seeded.** The node's resonance pattern has qualities that echo
   the temporal distortions [[dallid]] knows about from the [[briarwood-anomaly-map]].
   The spellbook reacts when the map is near the node — not the same reaction as the
   scored marks, but related. The connection between anomalies becomes more than a
   hypothesis.

5. **Return and report.** [[dallid]] returns to the surface. [[brennan-cole]] is waiting.
   His desperation surfaces — not gated behind a check, but through his actions. He has
   been preparing for the possibility that the investigation fails.

6. **The decision.** What does [[dallid]] do with what he found? The session ends on a
   decision point that sets up the next session's direction.

## Encounter Seeds

No combat encounters this session. The dead zone suppresses all magic; the Lattice node is
an environmental feature, not a combatant. The session is investigation, discovery, and
NPC interaction.

### The Node Chamber (situation)
- **What:** The center tunnel opens into a natural chamber — roughly20 feet across, low-
  ceilinged. The walls are rough stone, scooped out, unworked. In the centre, the stone
  is *wrong* — a distortion in the rock, a place where the air shimmers faintly, where
  the cold concentrates. The Lattice node.
- **Sensory details (varied from S2):** The air has a metallic taste — copper on the
  tongue. The stone underfoot vibrates at a frequency just below hearing, felt in the
  soles of the boots rather than heard. The shimmer in the air is visible only at the
  edges of perception — a flicker, like heat haze, but cold. The gecko's two heads track
  different aspects of the distortion: one follows the shimmer, the other follows a
  pattern in the stone that Dallid cannot see.
- **Lever:** The node is dying. The siphoning is draining it. If the drain continues, the
  node will collapse — and the dead zone will expand. This is the ticking clock the player
  may choose to act on.
- **Complication:** The evidence of exploitation is clear but anonymous. No names, no
  insignia, no tools left behind — only the marks of sustained, careful work. Whoever did
  this knew what they were doing and took precautions.

### The Tarin Trace (situation)
- **What:** Near the node, Dallid finds evidence that someone was here recently and left
  abruptly. Scuff marks in the dust. A miner's wrench — standard issue, the kind every
  worker carries. A spot where the dust is disturbed in a pattern that suggests someone was
  kneeling, then standing, then *gone*. The stone at one point is worn smooth — not from
  age, but from recent passage.
- **Lever:** The trace confirms Tarin reached the node. It does not answer where he went.
  The worn spot suggests the node itself transported him — a side effect of the siphoning
  that was not intended.
- **Complication:** The trace is old enough (nine days) that dust has settled over it.
  Dallid can reconstruct what happened but cannot reverse it. The trace is evidence, not a
  trail to follow.

## Discoveries & Investigations

### The Lattice Node
- **Committed answer:** The Lattice node is a natural formation — a point where the
  Primordial Lattice, the hidden architecture of reality, surfaces close to the material
  world. It manifests as a distortion in the stone: shimmer in the air, metallic taste in
  the air, vibration felt through the feet. The node has been here for centuries — the
  mineral deposits around it are Lattice energy crystallized in the rock.
- **What Dallid perceives:** His spellbook's resonance is the node's energy interacting with
  the Awakened Spellbook's consciousness. The book can sense the node directly; Dallid
  perceives it through the book's reactions and through his own unusual sensitivity (his
  altered biology from Erasmus's experiments, which the player does not yet understand the
  source of). In the dead zone, spells fail — but the node's own energy is present and
  perceivable to someone attuned to it. Dallid is attuned.
- **What the node reveals:** The node is being *drained* — not depleted naturally, but
  siphoned through engineered channels carved into the stone. The channels are deliberate:
  precise, geometric, following invisible Lattice pathways. The scored marks at the second
  crossing are the surface expression of these channels. Someone has been extracting Lattice
  energy from the node, pulling it down into the earth and away — this is why magic fails in
  the mine. The energy isn't gone; it's being redirected.
- **DC:** No check required to perceive the node — it is visible to Dallid through the
  spellbook's reactions and his own sensitivity. Investigation DC14 to examine the
  siphoning channels and determine they are engineered (the geometric patterns, the tool
  marks, the residue of extraction). Arcana DC15 to understand the channels follow Lattice
  pathways and the scored marks are the surface expression of the same network.

### Evidence of Deliberate Exploitation
- **Committed answer:** The siphoning has been ongoing for months — the channel carvings
  are fresh, the residue of extraction is recent. Whoever did this returned multiple times.
  The work is precise: they know what the Lattice is and how to interact with it. The
  channels follow invisible pathways that only someone who can perceive Lattice energy could
  trace — this explains the scratches at the first crossing and the scored marks at the
  second.
- **What Dallid finds:**
  - The channels are carved with something sharp but not a tool — the edges are too clean
    for metal, too precise for natural erosion. Something sharp that followed the invisible
    lines.
  - Residue in the channels: a faint, silvery substance that clings to the stone. It has
    the same metallic taste as the air. It is Lattice energy in semi-material form —
    extracted and left behind when the drain pulls the energy away.
  - No tools, no equipment, no personal effects. Whoever did this was careful. The only
    evidence is the marks they left in the stone.
- **Check:** Investigation DC14 to examine the channels and determine they are engineered.
  Arcana DC15 to understand the residue is Lattice energy in material form.

### Tarin's Trace
- **Committed answer:** Tarin stumbled into the node during a tunnel collapse nine days ago.
  He was kneeling near the node when the siphoning surge transported him — an unintended
  side effect. He is alive in the [[ashenmere-wastes]], confused but unharmed.
- **What Dallid finds:** Scuff marks in the dust near the node. A miner's wrench (standard
  issue). A spot where someone knelt, then stood, then the dust is *absent* — cleared in a
  pattern that suggests sudden displacement, not walking away. The stone at one point is worn
  smooth in a rough humanoid shape — as if someone pressed against it and passed through.
- **Check:** Investigation DC13 to reconstruct the sequence: someone was here, knelt near
  the node, and was suddenly removed. The worn spot suggests the node itself transported
  them. No body, no blood, no signs of violence — just absence.

### Briarwood Connection
- **Committed answer:** The anomalies are connected. The same force (the Unraveler) is
  exploiting Lattice nodes at both [[hollowfell-mine]] and [[the-briarwood]]. The
  node's resonance pattern shares qualities with the temporal distortions — the underlying
  mechanism is the same: Lattice energy being manipulated.
- **What Dallid perceives:** When he holds the [[briarwood-anomaly-map]] near the node (or
  thinks about the map's contents while standing at the node), the spellbook reacts
  differently — not the warmth of recognition it showed the scored marks, but a lower
  frequency, a deeper resonance. The map shows three anomaly sites; the node beneath
  Hollowfell is a fourth. The pattern of anomalies on the map and the pattern of the
  siphoning channels in the stone share a geometric structure — the same invisible
  pathways, expressed at different scales.
- **Check:** Arcana DC14 (or Keen Mind recall) to note the structural similarity between
  the map's anomaly clustering and the siphoning channels. This is a seed, not a
  confirmation — Dallid can hypothesize that the anomalies are connected, but he cannot
  prove it yet.
- **Note:** This is the first explicit connection between Hollowfell and the Briarwood. The
  player's hypothesis (from the knowledge ledger) is reinforced. Do not confirm the
  connection — suggest it. Let the player draw the conclusion.

## NPC Staging

### [[brennan-cole]]
- **At the surface works, waiting.** He has been waiting for hours. His disposition has
  shifted from grim-cooperative to grim-desperate.
- **What he knows:** The dead zone is artificial (learned from Dallid in S2). He does not
  know what Dallid found deeper in. He does not know about the node, the channels, or
  Tarin's trace.
- **His concealed thread:** He has been considering shutting down the mine without the
  consortium's permission. This session, his desperation surfaces through **actions**, not
  gated behind an Insight check:
  - When Dallid returns to the surface, he finds Brennan has gathered the workers. Not
    evacuating — but *preparing* to evacuate. Packed supplies near the bunkhouses. A
    conversation in progress about where to go. Brennan sees Dallid and stops — he wasn't
    going to leave without knowing what Dallid found, but he was getting ready to.
  - If Dallid shares what he found (the node, the siphoning), Brennan is relieved but
    grim: "Someone's been stealing from my mine. Can you stop them?" He will not enter the
    dead zone, but he will support whatever Dallid needs on the surface.
  - If Dallid does not share, or is evasive, Brennan's desperation hardens: "I can't keep
    them here. The consortium wants output and I've got nothing to give them. If you can't
    fix this, I'm pulling them out."
- **Disposition:** Relieved that someone found something. Grim about the implications.
  Willing to help on the surface. Will not enter the dead zone again.

### [[tarin]]
- **Not present.** alive in the [[ashenmere-wastes]], transported by the Lattice node.
  The trace at the node confirms he was here and was removed. Dallid does not know where
  he went — only that he reached the node and is no longer here.

## Decision Points

### 1. What does [[dallid]] do with the node?
- **Choice:** Study it further / Report to Brennan / Follow Tarin's trace / Leave the mine
  entirely.
- **What hangs on it:**
  - *Study further:* The node is dying. The siphoning continues. More study yields more
    detail about the channels and the residue, but the drain weakens the node further. If
    the node collapses, the dead zone expands and the evidence is lost.
  - *Report to Brennan:* Dallid returns to the surface with his findings. Brennan's
    desperation surfaces. The investigation gains a supporter on the surface but loses time
    at the node.
  - *Follow Tarin's trace:* The worn spot suggests the node transported Tarin. Dallid could
    attempt the same — but the node is unstable, the siphoning is ongoing, and the outcome
    is unpredictable. The trace does not indicate where Tarin went; only that the node
    moved him.
  - *Leave the mine:* Dallid exits with his findings. The investigation is paused. The node
    continues to drain. Brennan's desperation escalates.

### 2. Does [[dallid]] report to the [[aetherial-concordance]]?
- **Choice:** Send word to [[seraphine-duskward]] through the Concordance / Keep the
  findings to himself / Share with someone else.
- **What hangs on it:**
  - *Concordance:* Institutional resources, but institutional caution. The Concordance may
    send investigators, which could help — or could complicate things. [[seraphine-duskward]]
    has been named but not met. This could be the introduction.
  - *Keep to himself:* Dallid retains control of the investigation but loses potential
    support. The Unraveler's work continues unchecked.
  - *Share with someone else:* Whom? The player decides.

## Contingencies

### If [[dallid]] attempts magic at the node
Spells fail in the dead zone. The node's energy is present but the dead zone prevents
arcane manipulation. If Dallid attempts a spell, it fails as Dancing Lights did — not a
fizzle, the magic simply isn't there. However: the node's own energy is present. If Dallid
tries to interact with the node directly (touching it, reaching for it with his
sensitivity), the Awakened Spellbook may mediate — the book can perceive the node and
translate its energy. This is a narrative moment, not a mechanical one: the book warms,
the resonance intensifies, and Dallid perceives more of the node's structure. No spell is
cast; the book's consciousness bridges the gap.

### If [[dallid]] ignores the node and returns to the surface immediately
Brennan's desperation surfaces as described above. The investigation pauses. The node
continues to drain. The session becomes a surface-focused session: Brennan's thread,
reporting findings, deciding next steps. The node discovery is deferred to a future session
but the ticking clock (the node is dying) means the delay has consequences.

### If [[dallid]] attempts to follow Tarin's trace into the node
The worn spot suggests the node transported Tarin, but the mechanism is unstable. If
Dallid attempts to replicate the transport, the outcome is unpredictable — the node is
being drained, the energy is insufficient for controlled transport. The Awakened Spellbook
may resist (the book has a relationship with Dallid and may not want him to take the
risk). If Dallid persists, the outcome is determined by the runner at the table — but the
plan commits that the transport would be uncontrolled and dangerous, not a clean
teleportation. This is not the intended path; the plan expects Dallid to investigate and
return to the surface.

## Pacing & Exits

### Expected scope
3–3.5 hours. The session resolves the cliffhanger immediately (first10–15 minutes),
delivers the Lattice node discovery as the centrepiece (30–45 minutes of investigation),
returns to the surface for Brennan's thread and the decision point (30–45 minutes), and
closes on a clear choice.

### Natural stopping points
- **After the node discovery:** If the investigation runs long, the session can end with
  Dallid at the node, having found the source but not yet returned to the surface. The
  cliffhanger for S4 is the decision of what to do next.
- **After the surface return:** If pacing is tight, the session can end after Dallid
  reports to Brennan and the player faces the decision point. The cliffhanger for S4 is
  the choice itself.

### Session-end advancement check
Dallid is level 5. The major arc's level pacing schedules L5→L6 at ~S3, after
[[the-hollowfell-investigation]] concludes. The minor arc does not conclude this session —
the node is found but the investigation's implications are still unfolding. **No level-up
at S3 close.** L5→L6 lands when the minor arc reaches falling action (likely S4, after
Dallid reports his findings and the immediate situation is contained).
