# The Ashenmere Wastes

## Vitals
- **Kind / scale:** Blighted region — a vast scar on the land, roughly the size of a kingdom.
  Uninhabited by choice; only the desperate or the foolhardy enter.
- **Terrain:** Ash-grey soil, petrified forests, geysers of planar energy, pockets where
  gravity or time behaves incorrectly. No climate — the Wastes generate their own weather,
  which is invariably hostile.
- **Seat of power:** None — the Wastes are ungoverned and ungovernable. The Concordance
  monitors the perimeter.
- **Population:** None permanent. Scattered research camps on the edges; occasional expeditions
  into the interior.

## Geography

The Ashenmere Wastes are the largest surviving scar of the Sundering. Eight hundred years
ago, a coalition of archmages attempted to rewrite reality and failed; the Wastes are what
they left behind. The terrain is wrong in fundamental ways: stone flows like water in some
areas, gravity shifts without warning in others, and the dead sometimes walk — not as
undead, but as temporal echoes, replaying their final moments. The deeper one goes, the
worse it gets. No expedition has reached the centre and returned.

## Holdings / control

No faction controls the Wastes. The Concordance maintains monitoring stations on the
perimeter and occasionally funds research expeditions. The Crown of Thorns has declared the
Wastes a no-go zone but lacks the ability to enforce it. Independent scavengers and
desperate folk sometimes cross the edges for salvage.

- [[the-bone-gardens]] — a region where petrified trees have formed into shapes that
  resemble living beings frozen mid-scream.

## Culture & people

There is no culture in the Wastes — only the aftermath of catastrophe. The few people who
spend time here are researchers, scavengers, or the mad. The Concordance's perimeter camps
are spartan and functional; researchers rotate through on short assignments.

## Regional history

The Wastes were created during the Sundering, when the coalition of archmages attempted their
catastrophic Lattice manipulation. The blast radius was enormous — entire kingdoms were
destroyed in moments. The Wastes have been slowly healing ever since, but the process is
measured in centuries, not decades. The planar bleed — the leaking of energy from other
planes through the damaged Veil — has never fully stopped.

## Deeper layer / what's hidden here

- [hidden] The Wastes are not merely a scar — they are a wound in the Lattice itself. The
  Unraveler's original manipulation of the archmages was designed to create exactly this
  kind of wound: a place where the Lattice is thin enough to be exploited directly. The
  Wastes are the Unraveler's laboratory, and they have been using it for centuries.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (full); [[thaleus-venn]] (does not know).

## Connections

Adjacent to the [[thornmere-marches]] (west). The Wastes are bordered by a ring of
abandoned settlements and Concordance monitoring stations.

## Secrets

- [hidden] Deep within the Wastes, at the approximate centre, there is a Lattice node that
  has been destabilized to the point of active collapse. If it fails completely, the collapse
  could cascade through the entire regional Lattice. This is what the Unraveler is working
  toward.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (full).
