# The Unraveling

## Premise

Dallid came to the Thornmere Marches to find his missing mentor, Erasmus Venn. But the
anomalies spreading across the region — temporal distortions, failing magic, planar bleed —
are connected to Erasmus's research, and to something far larger. As Dallid investigates,
he discovers that Erasmus was taken by a secret faction, that his research approached
knowledge of the Primordial Lattice — the hidden architecture of reality — and that someone
is actively exploiting the Lattice to reshape the world. The search for Erasmus becomes a
race to understand the Lattice before the Unraveler's plan reaches completion.

## Core conflict & stakes

**Personal:** Find Erasmus — alive, dead, or changed. What happened to him will define
Dallid's understanding of his own past and his own nature. Erasmus treated Dallid as a
subject; the Unraveler treats reality as raw material. Dallid must decide whether he is
like them, or different.

**World-shaking:** The Unraveler is destabilizing the Lattice. If the Lattice collapses,
reality itself fractures. The anomalies are the symptoms; the cause is deliberate
exploitation. The final experiment — a complete rewrite of the Lattice — will either
succeed (destroying reality as it exists) or fail (catastrophic collapse). Either way,
the cost is real.

**Moral:** Dallid must confront the difference between studying life and valuing it. The
arc forces him to choose: is he the kind of person who observes suffering, or the kind
who acts to stop it?

## Key NPCs & factions

- [[seraphine-duskward]] — Ally; the PC's primary contact. She wants to find the truth
  behind the anomalies. She can provide resources and information, but she is constrained
  by the Concordance's institutional caution.
  - **S1:** Named by [[wren]] as the First Warden who sent her to Greyhaven. [[dallid]]
    has not met her. She is now a known but unmet figure in the fiction.
- [[thaleus-venn]] — Ally; the only person studying the Lattice. He can provide knowledge
  but is physically vulnerable and dismissed by his own institution.
- [[vashra-keel]] — Antagonist; the Covenant's field commander. She will try to suppress
  Lattice knowledge by any means necessary. She is a threat to Dallid's investigation and
  to Erasmus's survival.
- [[erasmus-venn]] — The missing mentor. He is alive, imprisoned by the Covenant. His
  research contains fragments of Lattice understanding. He is not a good person — he
  treated Dallid as a subject — but he is the key to understanding what's happening.
- [[unraveler]] — The hidden antagonist. Exploiting the Lattice to reshape reality.
  Identity unknown for most of the arc.

## Turning points

1. **The Briarwood Investigation (Levels 5-6)** — Dallid investigates the Briarwood for
   the Concordance. He discovers the temporal disturbances are not random — they follow a
   pattern. He finds traces of Erasmus's research methods in the anomalies. This is the
   first confirmation that Erasmus is connected to the anomalies.
   - [hidden:the-unraveling-1] The Unraveler has been using the Briarwood as a testing ground. The traces
     Dallid finds are from Erasmus's research, which the Unraveler seized after the
     Covenant captured Erasmus.
   - Known to: [[unraveler]]; [[thaleus-venn]] (will recognize the methods when shown
     the data).

2. **Erasmus Found (Levels 7-8)** — Dallid discovers that Erasmus is alive, imprisoned by
   the [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]]. He is being forced to continue his research under
   duress. The Covenant is using Erasmus's knowledge to understand the anomalies — not to
   stop them, but to determine whether the Lattice can be sealed permanently.
   - [hidden:the-unraveling-2] Erasmus is alive and imprisoned. The Covenant has his research notes, which
     contain fragments of Lattice understanding.
   - Known to: [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]]; [[vashra-keel]].

3. **The Lattice Revealed (Levels 9-11)** — Dallid frees Erasmus (or fails to). Erasmus
   reveals the truth about the Lattice — or parts of it. Dallid learns that his own
   altered biology makes him uniquely sensitive to Lattice phenomena. The Unraveler
   becomes aware of Dallid and assesses him as a threat.
   - [hidden:the-unraveling-3] The Primordial Lattice is the hidden architecture of reality. It can be
     manipulated. The Unraveler is doing so. Dallid's altered biology allows him to
     perceive Lattice forces that others cannot.
   - Known to: [[thaleus-venn]]; [[unraveler]]; now [[dallid]] (partially).

4. **The Unraveler's Identity (Levels 12-13)** — Dallid discovers the Unraveler's identity
   or discovers that the Unraveler is not a person but a force — an echo of the original
   Sundering that has been manipulating events for centuries. The full scope of the
   Unraveler's plan becomes clear: a complete rewrite of the Lattice, which would destroy
   reality as it exists.
   - [hidden:the-unraveling-4] The Unraveler was the entity that manipulated the archmages into triggering
     the Sundering. The current anomalies are preparation for the final experiment. The
     Unraveler's goal is a new reality, designed from the ground up.
   - Known to: [[unraveler]]; now [[dallid]].

5. **The Final Experiment (Levels 14-20)** — The Unraveler initiates the final experiment.
   The Lattice destabilizes. Reality fractures. Dallid must use his understanding of the
   Lattice — and his unique sensitivity — to either stop the Unraveler or contain the
   damage. The resolution is not guaranteed; the cost is real. Erasmus may play a role —
   his research is the key to understanding the Lattice, but his methods and motives are
   suspect.
   - [hidden:the-unraveling-5] The final experiment will either succeed (rewriting reality) or fail
     (catastrophic collapse). Dallid's intervention determines which. The cost of stopping
     it may be high — Erasmus's knowledge, Dallid's altered biology, or something else
     entirely.
   - Known to: [[dallid]].

## The committed answers

- What happened to Erasmus? → He was captured by the [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] after
  they learned his research approached Lattice knowledge. He is alive, imprisoned, and
  forced to continue his research.
- Why did the Academy erase his records? → The Academy's founding charter instructs them
  to suppress Lattice research. The current leadership does not know this explicitly — they
  follow tradition — but the instruction shaped their response to Erasmus's work.
- Is Erasmus a good person? → No. He is brilliant, boundaryless, and without moral
  compass. He treated Dallid as a subject, not a person. He is not evil — he simply does
  not understand morality. He will help Dallid if it serves his research; he will not help
  if it doesn't.
- What is the Unraveler? → An entity — possibly not human — that has been exploiting the
  Lattice for centuries. It manipulated the archmages into triggering the Sundering, and it
  has been weakening the Lattice ever since. Its goal is a complete rewrite of reality.
- Can the Lattice be saved? → Yes, but the cost is real. The Unraveler's manipulation has
  weakened the Lattice to the point of instability. Stopping the final experiment requires
  either destroying the Unraveler's anchor point or stabilizing the Lattice through a
  counter-manipulation — which carries its own risks.

## Tension curve

- **Build-up (Levels 5-6):** Local anomalies. Investigation. The trail to Erasmus. The
  Briarwood investigation is the first turning point.
- **Rising action (Levels 7-8):** Erasmus found. The Covenant revealed. Stakes escalate.
  The second turning point.
- **Rising action (Levels 9-11):** The Lattice revealed. Dallid's unique nature. The
  Unraveler becomes aware. The third turning point.
- **Climax (Levels 12-13):** The Unraveler's identity and plan revealed. The world is in
  danger. The fourth turning point.
- **Falling action (Levels 14-17):** Preparing for the final confrontation. Gathering
  allies. Understanding the Lattice. The tension holds at a high level but the immediate
  crisis is past.
- **Resolution (Levels 18-20):** The final experiment. Dallid's choice. The cost. The
  fifth turning point.

## Engagement paths

- **Direct:** Dallid investigates the anomalies, follows the trail to Erasmus, confronts
  the Covenant, discovers the Lattice, and faces the Unraveler. This is the expected path
  — investigation leading to confrontation.
- **Indirect:** Dallid gets involved through Concordance work, stumbles into the larger
  truth through side investigations, and is pulled deeper by the anomalies' escalation.
  The anomalies force his hand even if he doesn't seek them out.
- **Avoidance:** If Dallid ignores the anomalies, they escalate on their own. The
  Unraveler's plan proceeds. The Briarwood gets worse. Hollowfell Mine collapses. The
  Wastes expand. Eventually, the problem comes to him — and the cost of inaction is higher.
- **Alternative solutions:** Dallid could ally with the Covenant (if he agrees that the
  Lattice should be sealed), work with the Unraveler (if he's convinced the rewrite is
  worth the cost), or find a third option (stabilizing the Lattice rather than destroying
  or rewriting it). The arc does not prescribe the solution; it commits the facts and
  leaves the path open.
