# The Aetherial Concordance

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Ancient order — part research institution, part investigative body, part peacekeeping
  force. Organized like a combination of a scholarly academy and an intelligence agency.
- **Scale:** Continental — chapters in every major city, with a central headquarters in the
  Crown of Thorns. Perhaps two thousand active members, plus support staff and informants.
- **Seat:** [[the-concordance-bastion]] in [[the-ashford-court]].
- **Leadership:** A council of five Wardens, each responsible for a domain of inquiry.
  Currently led by First Warden Seraphine Duskward.
- **Founded:** ~600 years ago, in the aftermath of the Sundering.

## Identity / Public face

The Concordance presents itself as a scholarly order dedicated to understanding and
protecting the world's magical and planar boundaries. They investigate anomalies, contain
planar incursions, and serve as neutral arbiters in disputes involving magic. They are
generally respected — seen as competent, if sometimes slow to act — and they cooperate
openly with the Crown and other authorities.

## Goals & methods

The Concordance's stated goal is to maintain the stability of the Veil and prevent another
Sundering. Their methods include monitoring anomalies, researching planar phenomena, training
investigators, and — when necessary — intervening directly to contain threats. They are
methodical, evidence-driven, and cautious. Their greatest weakness is institutional caution:
they move slowly, build consensus carefully, and sometimes miss threats that don't fit their
models.

## Structure & key members

The five Wardens each lead a division: Veil-Monitoring, Artifact Recovery, Planar Containment,
Research, and Field Investigation. Below them are teams of investigators, scholars, and
operatives. Key members include:
- [[seraphine-duskward]] — First Warden; the operational leader.
- [[thaleus-venn]] — Senior researcher in the Lattice Studies division (a division that barely
  exists, because the Lattice is not officially recognized as real by the institution).

## Reach & resources

The Concordance has agents in every major city, a network of informants, access to the
Crown's resources, and a substantial library of planar research. They can mobilize
investigative teams quickly but are slower to authorize military force. Their political
reach is significant but not absolute — they are respected, not feared.

## Deeper layer / true agenda

The Concordance was founded to protect the Lattice, but over the centuries, knowledge of
the Lattice has faded even within the institution. Most members understand the Veil and
planar boundaries but do not know the Lattice exists. The Lattice Studies division is
underfunded and dismissed as fringe scholarship. The Concordance's true purpose — protecting
the architecture of reality — has been obscured by its own institutional inertia.

## Relationships

- [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] — Former allies, now estranged. The Covenant split from
  the Concordance over the question of whether dangerous knowledge should be preserved or
  destroyed. They are rivals but not enemies — yet.
- [[crown-of-thorns]] — Formal alliance; the Crown provides political legitimacy and funding.
- Independent investigators — The Concordance occasionally hires freelance adventurers for
  deniable operations.

## Disposition toward the PC

The Concordance is resource-strapped and willing to hire capable outsiders. They will
approach the PC with work: investigate anomalies, retrieve artifacts, contain threats. They
are transactional — they pay well and expect competence. They will become more invested in
the PC as the anomalies escalate and the PC proves useful.

## Secrets

- [hidden] The Concordance's founders knew about the Primordial Lattice and designed the
  order to protect it. That knowledge was lost three generations ago when the last Warden
  who understood died without passing it on. The current leadership does not know the
  Lattice exists.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (has rediscovered fragments); [[unraveler]] (knows).
- [hidden] The Concordance's Veil-monitoring network has been detecting the anomalies, but
  their data is incomplete — the Unraveler has been subtly corrupting the monitoring stations
  closest to their operations, creating blind spots.
  Known to: [[unraveler]].
