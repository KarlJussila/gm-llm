# The Concordance Bastion

## Description

The Bastion is a fortress that pretends to be a library. The exterior is scholarly —
stone walls covered in ivy, arched windows, a grand entrance — but the interior is
designed for containment. The archive is vaulted and warded. The research wings are
segregated by clearance level. The central tower houses the Veil-monitoring equipment
and the Warden's offices. The whole compound hums with low-level protective magic.

## Significance

The Bastion is the operational centre for the Concordance's investigation of the anomalies.
It houses the monitoring network's central hub, the archive of planar research, and the
leadership team. Any serious investigation will eventually lead here.

## Notable features

- The Archive — a vast collection of planar research, anomaly reports, and historical
  records. Access is restricted by clearance level.
- The Monitoring Hub — the central station for the Veil-monitoring network. Data from
  outposts across the region flows here.
- The Warden's Tower — the leadership offices, including [[seraphine-duskward]]'s office.
- The Containment Wing — a secure area for dangerous artifacts and research subjects.

## Based here

- [[seraphine-duskward]] — First Warden, based here.
- Concordance researchers, investigators, and support staff.
- The archivist — a nameless figure who controls access to the archive.

## Hooks

- The PC may be summoned here for briefings on anomaly investigations.
- The archive contains information the PC needs but may require clearance to access.
- The Monitoring Hub has data on the anomalies that could be crucial — if it hasn't been
  corrupted.
- The Containment Wing holds artifacts that might be relevant to the investigation.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

  foundations contain Lattice-warding that the current leadership does not understand. The
  warding is failing as the Lattice destabilizes.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (has read the original plans); [[unraveler]] (knows).
