# Active Threads

## Starting threads

These are the leads Dallid begins with, drawn from his backstory and the opening situation.

### The Erasmus Venn trail
- **What:** Dallid came to the Marches to find his missing mentor, [[erasmus-venn]].
  The last lead pointed to [[the-briarwood]].
- **Status:** Cold. No one in Greyhaven has heard of Erasmus. The trail needs new leads.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-unraveling]] (major arc)

### The anomalies
- **What:** Strange phenomena are spreading across the [[thornmere-marches]] — temporal
  disturbances in the [[the-briarwood]], magic failing in [[hollowfell-mine]], scattered
  reports of planar bleed. The symptoms seem disconnected.
- **Status:** Active. The Concordance is investigating. Dallid has been asking about them.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-unraveling]] (major arc), [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)

### The Concordance job
- **What:** The [[aetherial-concordance]] is hiring freelance investigators. Wren, a
  Concordance operative, has approached Dallid with a job: investigate the magic-dead zone
  at [[hollowfell-mine]].
- **Status:** Offered. Dallid has not yet accepted.
- **Hooks into:** [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] (minor arc)
