# check-init

The code has checked layout, frontmatter, links, secret ids, arc coverage and initial state.
Review the campaign's meaning:

- **Coverage.** The world has its broad strokes: countries and powers, important cities, factions
  and how they stand with each other, faiths, a history with named events. The region has
  structure: geography, named settlements, who holds power. The vicinity where the PC begins is
  populated: named people with wants, places to go, something going on, problems of its own beside
  the main story. Flag a tier left as generalities — a power, people or faith mentioned but never
  named, a faction with no one running it, a town with nobody in it.
- **Casting.** The arcs stand on named NPCs. Flag an arc role filled by an office or a crowd.
- **Duplicates.** Entities that duplicate another's name or role and should be one entity.
- **Consistency.** Compare the world, the primer, the PC and the arcs with `campaign/campaign.md`
  and each other. Flag contradictions in facts, tone, themes or the player's content boundaries,
  and anything in the primer that a hidden layer keeps from the player or the knowledge ledger
  doesn't give the PC.

Report each gap with its file, what conflicts or is missing, and the correction it needs.
