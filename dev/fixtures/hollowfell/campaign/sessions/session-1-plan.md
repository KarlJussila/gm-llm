# Session 1 — The Hook

## Opening State

**Where:** [[greyhaven]], [[thornmere-marches]]. The Rusty Anchor tavern — timber-framed,
peat smoke, river mud. Early evening, late autumn. The first frosts have arrived.

**When:** Dallid has been in Greyhaven for three days, staying at the Rusty Anchor while he
gets his bearings. He has been asking around about the anomalies and about
[[erasmus-venn]].

**Who's present:**
- [[dallid]], at a corner table with his spellbook and a half-eaten meal.
- [[maret]], the tavern keeper — a weathered woman behind the bar.
- A handful of locals: dockworkers, a few Marcher folk, a traveling merchant.
- [[wren]], a Concordance investigator — a young human woman sitting near the door,
  watching the room with professional attention.

**The situation:** Dallid has learned the basics: the Concordance has an outpost here, the
anomalies are getting worse, and the locals are scared. He has been asking about Erasmus Venn
and getting nowhere — no one in Greyhaven has heard of him. The Briarwood is a day's travel
east, and the Concordance is hiring investigators.

**The opening hook:** Wren approaches Dallid's table and asks if he's the one who's been
asking about anomalies and missing researchers. She has a job: the mining consortium at
[[hollowfell-mine]] needs someone to find out why magic fails in the deeper tunnels. The
Concordance is paying. Is he interested? This is the [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] arc's
entry point.

---

## Arcs to Advance

### The Hollowfell Investigation (minor) — ENTRY POINT

This session introduces the minor arc. Wren's job offer is the hook. The session ends with
Dallid committed to investigating Hollowfell Mine. The mine's deeper mysteries — the Lattice
node, the Unraveler's siphoning — are held for later sessions.

**What advances:** Dallid accepts the job and travels to the mine. The arc moves from
dormant to active.

### The Unraveling (major) — BACKGROUND THREAD

The major arc is not directly advanced this session. Dallid's search for Erasmus Venn
remains cold — no one in Greyhaven has heard of him. This is intentional: the Erasmus trail
stays dormant until the Briarwood investigation (turning point 1, levels 5-6). The
Hollowfell investigation will later reveal evidence that connects to the larger pattern, but
that connection is not made in session 1.

**What advances:** Nothing directly. The Erasmus thread remains cold. The anomalies thread
is touched (Dallid has been asking about them) but not advanced.

---

## Likely Beats

1. **The hook lands.** Wren approaches Dallid with the Hollowfell job. The offer is clear:
   go to the mine, find out why magic fails, report back. The Concordance pays.
2. **Dallid learns the basics.** Through Wren (and possibly [[maret]]), Dallid learns:
   the mine's deeper tunnels have a magic-dead zone, a worker disappeared last week, the
   mining consortium is losing money and workers are scared. The Concordance has no one
   available to investigate.
3. **The decision.** Dallid decides whether to take the job. This is the session's central
   choice — not whether to investigate anomalies (the campaign assumes he will) but how he
   approaches his first real work in the Marches.
4. **Travel and arrival.** If Dallid accepts, he travels to Hollowfell Mine. The journey
   establishes the Marches' terrain — marsh, bramble, foothills. Arrival at the mine
   establishes the setting: functional but spartan, workers hard and wary, the deep tunnels
   cold and quiet.
5. **First impression.** Dallid enters the mine or speaks with [[brennan-cole]], the
   foreman. The magic-dead zone is palpable — spells fizzle, magical items feel inert. The
   Awakened Spellbook reacts to something it cannot explain. This is the session's closing
   hook: something is wrong here that is not natural.

## Encounter Seeds

### 1. The Tavern Pitch (social)

**Type:** Social.
**Situation:** Wren approaches Dallid at the Rusty Anchor and offers the Hollowfell job.
**Lever:** Wren is competent but operating outside official channels — Seraphine Duskward
sent her, not the Concordance council. She needs Dallid to say yes; she can't go herself.
**Complication:** Dallid may push for more information than Wren has. She knows the
surface facts but not the deeper truth. She can describe the symptoms but not the cause.

**NPCs in scene:**
- [[wren]] — disposition: transactional but hopeful; agenda: fill the job, get a capable
  investigator to Hollowfell.
- [[maret]] — disposition: neutral; agenda: keep the peace, maybe offer local color.

### 2. Arrival at Hollowfell (exploration)

**Type:** Exploration.
**Situation:** Dallid arrives at Hollowfell Mine and takes in the scene — the surface works,
the workers, the entrance to the deep tunnels. The foreman [[brennan-cole]] is available
for a briefing.
**Lever:** The magic-dead zone is a physical presence. Dallid's Spellbook reacts — it
"sees" something Dallid cannot perceive directly. The reaction is the first clue that the
problem is not natural.
**Complication:** The workers are wary of outsiders and magic-users. Brennan Cole is
overwhelmed and may not volunteer information unless asked directly.

## Discoveries & Investigations

### The Magic-Dead Zone (check-gated, partial)

**The conclusion:** Something beneath Hollowfell Mine is draining magical energy — not a
natural depletion, but an active siphon. The Spellbook can sense the drain.

**The pieces:**
1. Dallid's spells fizzle in the deep tunnels. He can attempt an **Arcana check** to
   determine whether the dead zone is natural or artificial. On success: the character
   recognizes the signature of active magical interference, not mere absence. The zone has
   edges — it's not ambient, it's focused. *(new reveal, character-assembled)*
2. The Awakened Spellbook reacts to something in the deeper tunnels — it "sees" a
   presence, a pull, a wrongness that Dallid cannot perceive directly. The Spellbook's
   reaction is involuntary and persistent. *(new reveal, player-assembled — the player
   observes the book's behavior)*
3. Brennan Cole reports: a worker named [[tarin]] disappeared in the deep tunnels last
   week. No body was found. The other workers won't go deep anymore. *(known to:
   [[brennan-cole]]; new to [[dallid]])*

**How they combine:** The dead zone is artificial, the Spellbook senses something beneath
the mine, and a worker vanished into the deep tunnels. Together these point to an active
force at work — but the full picture (the Lattice node, the Unraveler's siphoning) is held
for later sessions. This session delivers only the first thread: something is wrong here,
and it's not natural.

**Knowledge flags:**
- Dead zone is artificial: *(new reveal, gated behind Arcana DC 13)*
- Spellbook reaction: *(new reveal, player-assembled from narration)*
- Tarin missing: *(new to [[dallid]])*

## NPC Staging

### Wren

**Disposition coming in:** Transactional but hopeful. She has been watching Dallid for a
day — he asks the right questions and he's capable. She needs someone to take the Hollowfell
job and she can't go herself (she's assigned to the Briarwood monitoring team).

**Agenda:** Fill the Hollowfell job. Get a capable investigator to the mine. Report back to
Seraphine.

**Knows / doesn't:**
- The mine's deeper tunnels have a magic-dead zone. *(known)*
- A worker disappeared last week. *(known)*
- The mining consortium is losing money. *(known)*
- The Concordance is stretched thin and can't send anyone. *(known)*
- Seraphine Duskward specifically asked her to recruit someone. *(known, partially — she
  won't volunteer that Seraphine sent her unless asked directly)*
- Why magic fails in the mine. *(doesn't know)*
- The Lattice. *(doesn't know)*

**Voice anchor:** Young, professional, speaks in short sentences. Not unkind but not warm —
she's here to work.

**Reactions:**
- If Dallid asks about Erasmus: she's never heard the name. She can check with the
  Concordance archives if he wants, but it'll take time.
- If Dallid asks about the broader anomalies: she knows the Briarwood disturbances are
  getting worse, but that's her assignment, not Hollowfell. She can share what she knows
  but it's limited.
- If Dallid pushes for more detail about the mine: she gives what she has. She doesn't
  have much — the consortium reported the problem, the Concordance assessed it was worth
  investigating, and she was told to hire someone.

### Maret

**Disposition coming in:** Neutral. Dallid is a paying customer who asks a lot of questions.
She's not hostile, not helpful — she runs a tavern, not an information bureau.

**Agenda:** Keep the peace. Serve drinks. Maybe gossip if the mood is right.

**Knows / doesn't:**
- The local rumors: the Briarwood is getting worse, Hollowfell has problems, strange
  travelers have been heading east. *(known)*
- The deeper political picture. *(doesn't know)*
- Erasmus Venn. *(doesn't know — nobody in Greyhaven does)*

**Voice anchor:** Weathered, dry, speaks in short declarative sentences. Not unfriendly but
not a source of free information.

**Reactions:**
- If Dallid asks about anomalies: she'll share the rumors. "The Briarwood's been wrong
  for months. People go in and come out... different. Or don't come out at all."
- If Dallid asks about Hollowfell: "Mine's got problems. Workers are scared. Magic doesn't
  work down there, or so they say."
- If Dallid asks about Erasmus: she doesn't know the name. "Never heard of him. You're
  the third person this week asking about someone nobody's heard of."

### Brennan Cole

**Disposition coming in:** Overwhelmed and pragmatic. He wants the mine working again. He
doesn't care about the Concordance or anomalies — he cares about output and keeping his
workers alive. He is not a bad person; he's in over his head and knows it.

**Agenda:** Get help. Keep the mine operational. Don't lose any more workers.

**Knows / doesn't:**
- The deep tunnels have a magic-dead zone. Spells fail beyond a certain depth. *(known)*
- A worker named [[tarin]] disappeared last week. No body found. *(known)*
- The mineral seam is declining. Output is down. *(known)*
- Workers are reporting strange sounds from deeper in. *(known)*
- Why any of this is happening. *(doesn't know)*

**Voice anchor:** Tired, blunt, practical. Speaks in short sentences. Doesn't volunteer
information — answers what's asked and nothing more.

**Reactions:**
- If Dallid asks about the dead zone: "Spells don't work past the second crossing. Wards
  fail. Even the cantrips. I've got a wizard's word for it — it's not natural."
- If Dallid asks about Tarin: "Went down two weeks ago. Didn't come back. We searched
  for three days. Found nothing. The tunnel was solid — no collapse, no cave-in. He just
  vanished."
- If Dallid asks about the strange sounds: "Groaning. Like the mountain's breathing. The
  deep workers hear it. I don't, but I believe them."
- If Dallid offers to investigate: relief. "Thank the gods. I've been paying out of my own
  pocket for the Concordance to send someone."

## Decision Points

### 1. Accept or decline the Hollowfell job

**What hangs on it:** Accepting begins the [[the-hollowfell-investigation]] arc. Declining
leaves the job unfilled — the mine's problems worsen, workers are at risk, and the first
thread of the larger mystery goes unpulled. Wren will press; Seraphine will eventually
intervene. But the immediate consequence of decline is delay and escalating local danger.

**Don't plan which option.** The session opens on the hook and follows the player's choice.

### 2. How to approach the investigation

**What hangs on it:** Dallid can investigate the mine systematically (asking questions,
examining the dead zone, talking to workers) or go straight to the deep tunnels. The first
path yields more context but slower progress. The second path is faster but risks
confronting the unknown without preparation.

---

## Contingencies

### If Dallid declines the Hollowfell job

Wren presses — the pay is good, the work is straightforward, and no one else is available.
She can reveal that Seraphine Duskward specifically requested a capable investigator, which
carries weight if Dallid knows who Seraphine is. If Dallid still declines, the session
shifts to exploration of Greyhaven and the anomalies — the Briarwood, the Concordance
outpost, the local rumors. The Hollowfell hook remains available for the next session, but
the mine's problems escalate in the interim.

### If Dallid investigates before accepting

Dallid can visit the Concordance outpost, question locals, or examine what data exists on
the anomalies. He learns surface-level information: the Briarwood disturbances are getting
worse, Hollowfell has problems, and the Concordance is stretched thin. He does not learn
the deeper truth (the Lattice, the Unraveler) — that requires field investigation. The
session can end with Dallid more informed but still facing the same decision: take the job
or don't.

### If Dallid pushes for details Wren doesn't have

Wren is honest about the limits of her knowledge. She can describe symptoms but not causes.
She can offer to connect Dallid with the Concordance's research division (Thaleus Venn) for
a theoretical perspective, but that's a referral for later — Thaleus is in the capital. If
Dallid demands answers before acting, Wren acknowledges she doesn't have them and asks
whether he's the kind of person who needs answers before acting, or the kind who goes to
find them.

## Pacing & Exits

### Expected scope

2-3 scenes. The session's center of gravity is the tavern scene — the hook, the decision,
and the departure. Travel to Hollowfell and arrival are the session's closing material.

### Exit conditions

**Primary exit: Dallid accepts the Hollowfell job and arrives at the mine.** The session
lands with Dallid standing at the entrance to the deep tunnels, the magic-dead zone
palpable, the Spellbook stirring. This is a satisfying stop — a decision made, a new
location established, a mystery opened.

**Cliffhanger:** The Awakened Spellbook reacts to something in the deeper tunnels. Dallid
can't perceive it directly, but the book's reaction is involuntary and persistent. Something
is wrong here that is not natural.

**Secondary exit: Dallid declines or delays.** The session lands in Greyhaven with the job
still on the table and the anomalies still escalating. Less satisfying but valid — the hook
is still live.

### Candidate landings

- **Landing A (primary):** Dallid arrives at Hollowfell Mine, enters the deep tunnels, and
  experiences the magic-dead zone. The Spellbook reacts. Session ends on the cliffhanger.
  Substance: the tavern scene, travel, and the first encounter with the mine's mystery.
- **Landing B (secondary):** Dallid accepts the job but hasn't yet departed. Session ends
  at the Rusty Anchor with the decision made and the journey ahead. Less substance but
  valid if the tavern scene runs long.

### Session-end advancement

**No level-up.** The major arc's first level-up (L5 → L6) is due ~S2, after the Briarwood
investigation (turning point 1). Session 1 is the opening beat — no advancement this session.
