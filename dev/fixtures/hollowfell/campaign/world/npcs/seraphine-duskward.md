# Seraphine Duskward

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~8 (a seasoned investigator with minor magical ability)
- **Ability scores:** STR 10 · DEX 13 · CON 12 · INT 16 · WIS 15 · CHA 14
- **Proficiencies:** Investigation, Insight, Arcana, Deception, Persuasion

## Abilities
Seraphine is a trained investigator with rudimentary divination magic — she can sense
magical disturbances and read the residue of planar activity. She carries a Concordance
badge that grants limited authority and a set of investigation tools.

## Identity / Public role

Seraphine presents as a composed, authoritative figure — the public face of the Concordance's
investigative arm. She is well-known in academic and political circles, respected for her
competence and her willingness to get her hands dirty. She dresses practically and speaks
directly; she has no patience for politics but is skilled at navigating it.

## Personality / mannerisms

Controlled, analytical, quietly intense. She listens more than she speaks and asks precise
questions. She has a dry sense of humor that surfaces rarely and a temper she keeps under
strict control. She is haunted by the anomalies — she knows something is wrong and she
cannot prove it, and that gap between knowledge and evidence gnaws at her.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Seraphine is genuinely dedicated to the Concordance's mission, but she is privately
frustrated by the institution's caution. She suspects the anomalies are connected and
that a single agent is responsible, but she lacks the evidence to convince the council.
She is taking risks — unauthorized investigations, freelance hires — that could cost her
position if discovered. She is also beginning to suspect that the Concordance's founders
knew something the current leadership does not.

## Agenda / what they want

To find the truth behind the anomalies before they escalate beyond containment. She wants
to prove that the anomalies are connected and that someone is responsible. She also wants
to understand why the Concordance's own data is incomplete — she suspects institutional
failure, not just bad luck.

## What they know

- The anomalies are increasing in frequency and severity.
- The monitoring network has blind spots that she cannot explain.
- The [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] is active again, which worries her.
- She has read fragments of the Concordance's founding charter that reference "the Lattice"
  but does not understand what it means.

## Disposition toward the PC

Transactional but hopeful. She will hire the PC for investigation work — anomaly assessment,
artifact recovery, containment operations. She is looking for capable outsiders who can
operate independently and report back. She will become a mentor figure if the PC proves
reliable, sharing information and resources that a stranger would not receive.

## Secrets

- [hidden:seraphine-duskward-1] She has been conducting unauthorized investigations into the Concordance's blind
  spots and suspects someone inside the institution is compromised.
  Known to: [[seraphine-duskward]] only.
- [hidden:seraphine-duskward-2] She has requested a copy of the original founding charter from the archives and
  been denied. She does not yet know why.
  Known to: [[seraphine-duskward]]; the Concordance archivist.
