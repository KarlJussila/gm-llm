# The Ashford Court

## Vitals
- **Kind:** Capital city — seat of the [[crown-of-thorns]] monarchy.
- **Region:** [[crown-of-thorns]]
- **Setting:** A large city on the River Ash, in the heart of the kingdom.
- **Controlled by:** [[crown-of-thorns]]
- **Scale:** Perhaps fifty thousand residents. A walled city with a castle, a cathedral,
  a merchant quarter, and a scholarly district.

## Description

The Ashford Court is a city of stone and river-smoke. The castle dominates the skyline —
a grey fortress on a hill — while the city spreads below in a maze of streets and markets.
The scholarly district houses the [[the-concordance-bastion]] and several minor academies.
The air smells of river water and cooking fires.

## Significance

The capital is the political and economic centre of the kingdom. Every faction has a
presence here. The Concordance Bastion is the regional headquarters for anomaly
investigation.

## Hooks

- The PC may be summoned here for briefings.
- The archive at the Bastion holds information the PC needs.
- The court is divided on how to respond to the anomalies.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden] The city's foundations contain Lattice-wards placed during the original
  construction — ancient protections that the current inhabitants do not understand. The
  wards are failing as the Lattice destabilizes, and the Crown's prosperity — which
  depends on the wards' stabilizing effect — is beginning to erode. Queen Isolde has
  noticed the decline but cannot explain it.
  Known to: [[unraveler]] (knows and is exploiting); Queen Isolde (suspects something is
  wrong but lacks the knowledge to diagnose it).
