**Session 1.** Three days in the Rusty Anchor in Greyhaven, asking after Erasmus Venn, got you
nothing but blank looks. Then Wren — a young Concordance field investigator — sat down at your
table with a job: find out why magic fails in the deep tunnels of Hollowfell Mine, a few hours east
in the foothills, where a worker vanished last week. Her own assignment is the Briarwood, where time
runs wrong and a research team of four went in toward the Echo Glade three days ago and hasn't come
back. She didn't know Venn's name. You told her he was your mentor and that his trail leads into the
Briarwood, and you took the Hollowfell job. She gave you her map of the Briarwood's three anomaly
sites — the Deep Paths, the Echo Glade and the Thornwall — asked to hear anything you find that ties
Hollowfell to the forest, and told you to report to Brennan Cole at the mine. First Warden Seraphine
Duskward of the Concordance sent her; anything that matters should reach Duskward. You settled your
tab with Maret, the tavern keeper, and walked the old mining road that night. At the tunnel mouth
the air felt hollowed out, magic simply stopped, and your spellbook began to stir, sensing something
in the dark.

**Session 2.** Brennan Cole, the mine's tired foreman, told you that every spell fails past the
second crossing; that a miner, Tarin, went down the center tunnel nine days ago and never came back,
his lantern found still lit at the second crossing; and that workers hear a groaning from everywhere
at once. He guided you in. At the threshold you worked out (Arcana) that this is no natural dead
zone: the magic is being drained, deliberately, down into the earth, and your spellbook can feel
where it goes. At the first crossing you found fresh scratches, not made by miners, following
invisible lines in the stone; Brennan said none of his people made them. At the second crossing the
tunnel splits three ways, and your Dancing Lights didn't so much as spark. Scored lines there formed
a fuller pattern, which you sketched; held to the spellbook, it confirmed the pattern maps something
real beneath the mine. Brennan turned back for the surface. You went on down the center tunnel
alone, into a silence that swallows every sound, your gecko rigid on your shoulder and the
spellbook's resonance peaking. Something is waiting in the dark ahead. That is where you are.
