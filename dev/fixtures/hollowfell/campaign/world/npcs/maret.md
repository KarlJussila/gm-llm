None.

## Identity / Public role

Maret runs the Rusty Anchor — Greyhaven's main gathering place for dockworkers, Marcher
folk, merchants, and the occasional adventurer. She is a fixture: weathered, practical, and
permanently unimpressed. The tavern is hers and has been for twenty years. She knows
everyone who passes through and most of what they're carrying.

## Personality / mannerisms

Dry, unhurried, declarative. She answers questions with the minimum information required and
adds color only when the mood strikes. She has a memory for faces, names, and grudges. She
does not suffer fools but she does not seek conflict — she runs a tavern, not a courtroom.
Her default is neutrality: she serves drinks, maintains order, and shares rumors when it
suits her.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Maret is a practical woman with no hidden agenda. She has lived in Greyhaven long enough to
see the Marches change — the anomalies, the Concordance's growing presence, the fear in the
air — and she doesn't like it. She is not an informant or a spy; she is a tavern keeper who
observes because that is what tavern keepers do. If pushed, she will share what she knows,
but she will not go looking for trouble.

## Agenda / what they want

Keep the Rusty Anchor running. Serve her customers. Stay out of the anomalies. If trouble
comes to her door, she will deal with it — but she would rather it didn't.

## What they know

- The local rumors: the Briarwood is getting worse, Hollowfell has problems, strange
  travelers have been heading east. *(surface knowledge, common gossip)*
- The Concordance is hiring investigators. People have been coming through asking about
  anomalies. *(surface knowledge)*
- Erasmus Venn. *(does not know — nobody in Greyhaven does)*

## Disposition toward the PC

Neutral. Dallid is a paying customer who asks a lot of questions. She has no reason to help
or hinder him. If he buys drinks and isn't trouble, she'll share what she knows. If he
pushes too hard, she'll shut down — she answers questions, she doesn't get interrogated.

## Secrets

None. Maret has no hidden agenda, no concealed allegiance, and no secrets beyond the normal
gossip of a tavern keeper. She is what she appears to be.
