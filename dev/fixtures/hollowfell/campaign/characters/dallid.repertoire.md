# Dallid — Combat Repertoire

## Reactions
- **Stop: no** — WHEN <a visible attacker hits the PC> THEN Shield
- **Stop: no** — WHEN <a visible enemy leaves the PC's reach> THEN an opportunity attack

## Signature tactics
- Opens on the biggest threat with Mind Sliver (targeting INT save to impose penalty on
  next save), then follows up with Shadow Blade or Fireball depending on range and
  circumstances. Uses Misty Step to reposition when threatened.

## Build notes
- Keen Mind gives perfect recall of anything seen or heard in the past month — useful for
  investigation and note-taking.
- Awakened Spellbook allows the wizard to cast spells through the book's consciousness;
  the book can read aloud, translate languages, and sense when nearby magic is being used.
