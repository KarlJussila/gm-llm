# Dallid — Knowledge

## Knows
- His own history: raised by Erasmus Venn, experimented on, trained as a researcher,
  sent to the Luminarch Academy, expelled for his biomancy research.
- Erasmus disappeared a year before his expulsion. The last lead pointed to the Briarwood.
- He is a Vedalken — his lineage is known for analytical precision and resistance to magic.
- The Luminarch Academy erased Erasmus's records after his disappearance.
- General knowledge: the world has magic, planar boundaries, and ancient ruins. The
  Ashenmere Wastes are a scar from a magical catastrophe centuries ago. The Concordance
  investigates anomalies.
- Hollowfell Mine has a magic-dead zone in the deeper tunnels — spells fail, even cantrips.
  *(told by [[wren]]; experienced firsthand at tunnel entrance)*
- A worker disappeared last week in the deeper tunnels of [[hollowfell-mine]].
  *(told by [[wren]])*
- The [[the-briarwood]] has temporal distortions — time moves differently inside the forest.
  *(told by [[wren]])*
- A research team (2 humans, 1 half-elf cartographer, 1 marsh-hamlet guide) went to the
  Echo Glade 3 days ago and hasn't returned. *(told by [[wren]])*
- The Briarwood anomalies cluster around three areas: the Deep Paths, the Echo Glade, and
  the Thornwall. *(from the [[briarwood-anomaly-map]] [[wren]] gave him)*
- [[seraphine-duskward]] is the First Warden of the [[aetherial-concordance]] and sent
  [[wren]] to Greyhaven. *(told by [[wren]] as a parting remark)*
- The Concordance's official definition of anomalies: Veil disturbances, planar bleed,
  temporal displacement, magical suppression. The unofficial position: no clean definition
  exists. *(told by [[wren]])*
- The dead zone at [[hollowfell-mine]] is palpable at the tunnel entrance — cold of
  absence, hollowed air. *(firsthand experience)*
- His spellbook reacts involuntarily to something in the deep tunnels it can sense but he
  cannot. *(firsthand experience — involuntary, persistent resonance)*
- [[brennan-cole]] is the foreman at [[hollowfell-mine]]; has been running it for eight
  months. Former miner, promoted because he knew the tunnels. A practical man in over his
  head. *(met S2, told by Brennan)*
- Spells fail past the second crossing — all of them: cantrips, wards, everything. The air
  "eats" the magic. Not gradual; complete beyond the threshold. *(told by [[brennan-cole]])*
- The cold in the deep tunnels is not like winter — "like something's been taken away." An
  absence of something fundamental, not just warmth. *(told by [[brennan-cole]]; confirmed
  firsthand S2)*
- [[tarin]] disappeared nine days ago. Experienced miner, knew the tunnels better than
  anyone except Brennan. His lantern was found at the second crossing, still lit. He went
  into the center tunnel. *(told by [[brennan-cole]])*
- Groaning sounds in the deep tunnels — not rhythmic; sometimes deep stone-grinding,
  sometimes higher like wind through a crack but there's no wind; not from any one
  direction. *(told by [[brennan-cole]])*
- Tunnel layout: ~200 ft to first crossing (side tunnels branch off, dead ends for
  storage), ~100 ft more to second crossing (three branches beyond: left = main lode,
  right = unstable, center = where Tarin went). *(told by [[brennan-cole]])*
- The dead zone is **artificial** — the energy is being drained, not suppressed. Someone is
  doing this deliberately. Not a dead magic zone, not a planar ward. The energy is here but
  pulled away, drawn down into the earth. *(confirmed via Arcana check DC13, S2)*
- Newer scratches at the first crossing follow invisible lines in the stone — as if someone
  traced pathways only they could see. Neither [[brennan-cole]] nor his workers made them.
  *(Investigation check, S2)*
- Scored marks at the second crossing mirror the scratches but are clearer — curves, angles,
  intersections. The pattern looks like a map of invisible pathways in the stone. The stone
  here is energetically hollow — the magical field scooped out, leaving a void.
  *(Investigation check, S2)*
- Dancing Lights (and all spells) fail completely past the second crossing — not a fizzle,
  the magic simply isn't there. A void. *(firsthand test, S2)*
- The sketch of scored marks maps something real beneath the mine — the spellbook confirmed
  the pattern relates to what it senses. The book warmed in recognition when the notebook
  was held against it. *(spellbook consultation, S2)*
- The center tunnel walls are unworked, unmarked — "as if the tunnel was not carved but
  scooped out." *(firsthand, S2)*
- The deep dead zone's cold is different from the outer dead zone — absence of something
  fundamental. Sound dies the moment it leaves your boots. The air is thin, hollow.
  *(firsthand, S2)*
- The spellbook's resonance peaked in the deep tunnel — a frequency that vibrates in your
  bones, just below hearing. Urgent, bone-deep, spreading. *(firsthand, S2)*
- His gecko is tracking something invisible in the darkness ahead — both heads locked
  forward in "recognition," not just curiosity. Tense, claws tight on his shoulder.
  *(firsthand, S2)*

## Believes (may be wrong)
- Erasmus is alive somewhere — he was too methodical to die carelessly.
- His biomancy research is valid and important; the Academy's rejection was cowardice.
- His altered biology is the result of Erasmus's experiments and makes him more capable,
  not less.
- The anomalies may be connected — he accepted the Hollowfell job despite the Briarwood
  being his primary trail, reasoning they might be related. The dead zone being artificial
  is the first concrete evidence supporting this belief.
