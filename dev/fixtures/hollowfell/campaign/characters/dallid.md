## Appearance

Dallid is tall — well over six feet — with the hairless, blue-grey skin characteristic of
his lineage. His features are angular and precise, his movements deliberate. He dresses
practically: layered robes in muted colours, a leather satchel full of notes and samples,
and a worn spellbook he speaks to as though it were a person. His eyes are pale and
unblinking; he has a habit of studying people with the same intensity he applies to
specimens.

## Personality

Brilliant, analytically gifted, insatiably curious — and socially oblivious to the point of
unsettling. Dallid misses social cues, names uncomfortable truths, and treats people as
fascinating subjects of study. This isn't mere awkwardness; it's a learned inability to see
others as more than biological systems. He is earnest and transparent, incapable of
deception, and will say exactly what he's thinking in far more detail than anyone wanted.

Beneath the analytical shell is something unnamed — a loneliness he has no language for.
He doesn't know he's reaching for connection; he just does, clumsily. Rare moments surface
where the shell cracks and he protects or marvels at something for no useful reason.

## Backstory

Dallid was raised by a researcher named Erasmus Venn — a man who was, in every way that
mattered, like him: brilliant, boundaryless, and without a sense of morality or social
propriety. The relationship was never warm. Erasmus took Dallid off the streets as a child
not to help him, but to experiment on him. Dallid was a specimen first, an apprentice later,
and the dynamic barely shifted — he was promoted from subject to promising tool. He was
taught because he was useful to the research, not out of care.

Dallid doesn't understand that any of this was wrong. It was the only world he knew, and it
made him into the same kind of person. When he speaks of it at all, it's without resentment.

He was sent to the Luminarch Academy as a teenager — Erasmus's idea, to give him access to
resources and information. Dallid thrived intellectually but made no friends. His research
into "biomancy" — how living systems interact with underlying magical forces — attracted
attention. The Academy expelled him two years ago, calling his work dangerous.

Erasmus disappeared a year before the expulsion — vanished without explanation. The Academy
erased his records. Dallid came to the Thornmere Marches following the last lead he had:
Erasmus was heading toward the Briarwood.

## Character hooks (DM-side)

- **Erasmus's disappearance:** what really happened to him? Was he taken, did he flee, or
  did he find something in the Briarwood that changed him? Are there others who knew him
  — and are they allies or threats?
- **Dallid's altered biology:** Erasmus's experiments on Dallid left marks — his lineage is
  already unusual, but his biology has been further modified. This makes him uniquely
  sensitive to Lattice phenomena, and potentially valuable to people who want to study or
  exploit that sensitivity.
- **The moral fault line:** an arc that forces Dallid to confront the difference between
  studying life and valuing it — and a real choice about whether he changes.
