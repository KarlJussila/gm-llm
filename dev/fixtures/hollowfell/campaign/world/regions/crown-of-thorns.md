# The Crown of Thorns

## Geography

The Crown of Thorns occupies the fertile heartland west of the Marches. The River Ash
runs through its centre, feeding farmland and connecting the major cities. The highlands
to the north are forested and sparsely populated; the coast to the south is dotted with
trading ports. The kingdom is well-connected by road and river, making it the commercial
hub of the region.

## Holdings / control

The Crown governs directly through a network of lords, governors, and appointed officials.
The court is functional but strained — succession disputes, border tensions with the
Marches, and economic pressure from the anomalies are all testing the monarchy. The
[[aetherial-concordance]] has a formal presence in the capital; the
[[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]] operates in the shadows.

- [[high-thorn-fortress]] — the Crown's military stronghold on the northern border.

## Culture & people

The Crown's culture is cosmopolitan but hierarchical. The court values learning, martial
skill, and political acumen. The common folk are prosperous enough — the farms produce, the
trades run, the cities are safe — but there is an undercurrent of anxiety. The anomalies
are disrupting trade routes, and the Crown's inability to explain or contain them is
eroding confidence.

## Regional history

The Crown of Thorns was founded four centuries ago by a coalition of Marcher Lords who
unified the western kingdoms against the Ashenmere threat. It has been stable ever since,
with a succession of capable monarchs and a functioning bureaucracy. The current crisis
is the first real test of the institution in living memory.
