## The Character

**What Dallid did:**
1. Engaged Wren at the Rusty Anchor when she approached with the Hollowfell job offer.
2. Asked probing questions about the job, the Briarwood, and Erasmus Venn.
3. Revealed that Erasmus Venn was his mentor and he's searching for him — this is new information Wren didn't have.
4. Accepted the Hollowfell job.
5. Received a hand-drawn map from Wren marking three Briarwood anomaly locations: the Deep Paths, the Echo Glade, the Thornwall.
6. Asked Maret about travel to Hollowfell (distance, safety).
7. Settled his tab and departed Greyhaven at night.
8. Traveled to Hollowfell Mine (2 hours).
9. Arrived at the mine entrance. Experienced the dead zone — the cold of absence, the hollowed air. His Spellbook reacted involuntarily to something in the deep tunnels.

**What Dallid learned:**
- Hollowfell Mine has a magic-dead zone in the deeper tunnels (surface facts from Wren)
- A worker disappeared last week (from Wren; Brennan Cole will provide details)
- The Briarwood has temporal distortions — time moves differently inside
- A research team went to the Echo Glade 3 days ago and hasn't returned
- The Concordance erased Erasmus's records (Dallid already knew this; Wren confirmed she doesn't know why)
- Seraphine Duskward is the First Warden and the one who sent Wren to Greyhaven
- The Concordance's definition of anomalies: officially, Veil disturbances, planar bleed, temporal displacement, magical suppression. Unofficially, no clean definition.

**What Dallid didn't learn:**
- Why magic fails in the dead zone (next session's investigation)
- What happened to Tarin (Brennan Cole will share)
- Anything about the Lattice or the Unraveler (held for later)
- Anything about Erasmus's current location or status (trail remains cold)

**Character development:**
- Dallid is analytical and precise — he asked detailed questions before committing
- He revealed personal information (Erasmus as mentor) to a stranger, which is notable for someone who treats people as subjects of study
- His Spellbook's involuntary reaction is the first sign that something in Hollowfell is not natural — this is the session's closing hook and the investigation's entry point

## DM Notes

**What worked:**
- Wren's introduction — professional directness, slight crack when mentioning resource limitations
- Layered mysteries — Hollowfell vs. Briarwood, meaningful choices, information earned not dumped
- Spellbook cliffhanger — sensory details, clear hook
- The dead zone described through sensory experience, not mechanics

**What to adjust:**
- The map from Wren felt too convenient — she should have been more guarded, or offered it as a trade (Briarwood data for Hollowfell findings). Information should cost something, even from allies.
- Wren's willingness to share Briarwood details to a stranger should be more transactional — she's professional, not generous.

**Next session plan:**
- Session 2 should begin at Hollowfell Mine, with Dallid meeting Brennan Cole and investigating the dead zone
- The Arcana check (DC 13) is the session's first discovery beat
- Tarin's disappearance is the second beat — Brennan shares this
- The Spellbook's reaction deepens as Dallid enters the tunnels
- The session should end with the first turning point (The Investigation Begins) — Dallid experiences the dead zone firsthand, the Spellbook reacts, and the mystery opens
