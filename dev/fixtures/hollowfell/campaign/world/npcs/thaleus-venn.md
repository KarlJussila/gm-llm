# Thaleus Venn

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~6 (an elderly scholar with deep knowledge but limited physical capability)
- **Ability scores:** STR 8 · DEX 9 · CON 10 · INT 19 · WIS 16 · CHA 11
- **Proficiencies:** Arcana, Investigation, History, Religion

## Abilities
Thaleus is a brilliant researcher with no combat ability. His value is entirely in his
knowledge and his ability to connect disparate pieces of information. He can read ancient
script, interpret planar phenomena, and has developed theoretical frameworks for the Lattice
that are decades ahead of the Concordance's official position.

## Identity / Public role

Thaleus is a marginal figure in the Concordance — respected for his intellect but dismissed
for his obsession. He has spent forty years researching "the Lattice," a concept the
institution considers theoretical at best and dangerous at worst. His division has three
members and a basement office. He is seen as a harmless eccentric by most of his colleagues.

## Personality / mannerisms

Eccentric, intense, prone to long tangents. He speaks in rapid bursts when excited and
goes quiet when processing. He carries everywhere a battered leather satchel full of
notes and diagrams. He is kind but distracted, often forgetting social niceties. He
trusts evidence over authority and is stubbornly independent.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Thaleus has spent forty years piecing together the Lattice's existence from fragments:
ancient texts, anomaly data, his own theoretical work. He is closer to the truth than
anyone alive — he knows the Lattice is real, he knows the anomalies are connected, and
he suspects a single agent is responsible. What he does not know is the Unraveler's
identity or the full scope of their plan. He is also unaware that the Unraveler is aware
of him and has been working to discredit his research.

## Agenda / what they want

To prove the Lattice exists and that the anomalies are symptoms of its destabilization.
He wants the Concordance to take his research seriously and act on it. He is also looking
for field evidence — someone who has experienced the anomalies firsthand and can provide
data he cannot get from his desk.

## What they know

- The Primordial Lattice is real and underlies all of reality.
- The anomalies are connected and increasing.
- The Sundering was not a natural disaster — someone caused it.
- The Concordance's founding charter references the Lattice, but the current leadership
  does not know this.

## Disposition toward the PC

Grateful and eager. If the PC approaches him with anomaly data or field experience, he
will be intensely interested. He will share his research freely — perhaps too freely,
giving the PC more information than is safe. He is not a protector; he is a researcher
who has been starving for evidence and a collaborator.

## Secrets

- [hidden:thaleus-venn-1] He has discovered that the Unraveler — whoever they are — has been actively
  suppressing his research. He has found evidence of data corruption in the Concordance's
  archives and cannot explain it.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] only.
- [hidden:thaleus-venn-2] He suspects the Unraveler is connected to the original Sundering but has not
  been able to prove it. He is correct.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (suspicion, not certainty).
