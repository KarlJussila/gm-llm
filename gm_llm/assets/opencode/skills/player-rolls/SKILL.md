# player-rolls

Roll with the `dice` tool, add the modifier from your sheet, and report the total — noting
advantage or disadvantage if you rolled with it. The DM doesn't add the modifier; you do. Report
the number and nothing else: never narrate how the roll lands, whether you hit, the damage, or how
anything responds.
