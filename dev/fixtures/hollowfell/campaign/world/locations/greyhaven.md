# Greyhaven

## Description

Greyhaven is a town that has learned to live with water. The streets are raised wooden
boardwalks; the buildings are timber-framed and steeply roofed to shed the constant rain.
The market square floods twice a year, and everyone has a plan for it. The air smells of
peat smoke, river mud, and salt. The dock is the town's heart — barges come and go,
carrying peat, fish, and the occasional adventurer looking for work.

## Significance

Greyhaven is the PC's home base — the place where jobs are posted, supplies are bought,
and information is gathered. It is also the site of the [[aetherial-concordance]] outpost,
which is how the PC will eventually become involved in the larger investigation.

## Notable features

- The Rusty Anchor — the main tavern, where adventurers and merchants gather.
- The Concordance outpost — a small, understaffed office on the edge of town.
- The market square — the town's economic centre, flooded or not.
- The marsh-hamlets — scattered settlements in the surrounding bogs, home to the
  region's most independent folk.

## Who's here

- [[seraphine-duskward]] — visits periodically; currently stationed in the capital but
  has agents here.
- Concordance investigators — a small team monitoring anomalies in the Briarwood.
- Local merchants, Marsher folk, and the occasional adventurer.

## Hooks

- The Concordance is hiring investigators for anomaly-related work.
- The Briarwood's temporal distortions are getting worse, and locals are scared.
- Hollowfell Mine has been reopened, and workers report that magic doesn't work there.
- Strange travelers have been seen heading toward the Ashenmere Wastes.

## Deeper layer / what it hides

- [hidden:greyhaven-1] Greyhaven sits near a minor Lattice node — one of the reasons the Concordance
  established an outpost here. The node is stable but the Unraveler has been testing its
  boundaries.
  Known to: [[thaleus-venn]] (theoretical); [[unraveler]] (knows).

## Connections

Connected by river to the [[crown-of-thorns]] (west) and by road to the [[ashenmere-wastes]]
(east). The nearest significant location is [[the-briarwood]], a day's travel to the east.
