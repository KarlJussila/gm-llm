# Vashra Keel

## Vitals
- **Full name:** Vashra Keel
- **Race / lineage:** tiefling
- **Pronouns:** she/her
- **Age:** mid 40s
- **Role / station:** Field commander, [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]]
- **Affiliation:** [[covenant-of-the-sealed-veil]]

## Stats (rough sketch)
- **Equivalent level:** ~10 (a seasoned operative with magical training and combat capability)
- **Ability scores:** STR 12 · DEX 16 · CON 14 · INT 14 · WIS 13 · CHA 15
- **Proficiencies:** Stealth, Deception, Intimidation, Arcana, Insight

## Abilities
Vashra is a trained mage with a specialization in suppression and containment magic — spells
that neutralize magical effects, seal planar breaches, and render artifacts inert. She carries
Covenant-designed tools: nullification stones, planar anchors, and a set of enchanted blades
that disrupt magical defenses.

## Identity / Public role

Vashra operates under multiple identities. In public, she is a traveling merchant, a
scholar, a mercenary — whatever role serves the Covenant's immediate needs. She is
convincing in any disguise and can maintain a cover for weeks. Her true identity is
known only to the Veilkeepers and her closest operatives.

## Personality / mannerisms

Controlled, pragmatic, ruthless when necessary. She speaks precisely and wastes no words.
She is genuinely convinced that the Covenant's mission is righteous — that the Lattice is
dangerous and must be sealed — and she carries the weight of that conviction without
flinching. She is capable of kindness but does not show it readily; trust is a tool,
not a default.

## Deeper layer / true nature

Vashra believes she is saving the world. She has seen what the Lattice can do — she has
been to the Ashenmere Wastes and witnessed the damage firsthand — and she is convinced
that the only safe course is to suppress all knowledge of it. She is not wrong about the
danger; she is wrong about the solution. She does not know about the Unraveler's existence
and would be horrified to learn that someone is actively exploiting what she has spent
her life trying to contain.

## Agenda / what they want

Short term: suppress any investigation into the anomalies that might lead to Lattice
knowledge. Long term: locate and destroy any artifacts, texts, or individuals that pose
a threat to the Veil. She is willing to kill to achieve this, and she has done so before.

## What they know

- The Lattice is real and dangerous.
- The Concordance's founders knew about it and chose to suppress the knowledge.
- The anomalies are connected to the Lattice but she does not know the Unraveler is behind
  them.
- The Covenant has a contingency plan to seal the Lattice permanently.

## Disposition toward the PC

Varies depending on the PC's actions. If the PC is investigating the anomalies, she will
assess whether they pose a threat to the Covenant's goals. If they do, she will attempt
to redirect, discredit, or — as a last resort — eliminate them. If they don't, she will
ignore them. She is not cruel; she is efficient.

## Secrets

- [hidden] She has personally killed three researchers who got too close to Lattice knowledge.
  She considers these acts of necessity, not murder.
  Known to: [[vashra-keel]]; the Veilkeepers.
- [hidden] She does not know about the Unraveler and would be a potential ally if she did —
  her skills in suppression magic would be valuable against Lattice manipulation.
  Known to: [[vashra-keel]] only (unconscious ignorance).
